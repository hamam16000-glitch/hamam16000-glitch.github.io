What this calculator estimates

Estimate total startup funding needed from launch costs, inventory, deposits, working capital and contingency.

How to use it

Enter Formation, legal and licenses ($); Equipment and technology ($); Buildout or website development ($); Initial inventory and supplies ($); Launch marketing ($); Deposits and prepaid costs ($); Expected monthly overhead ($); Working-capital runway (months); Contingency on startup costs (%). Select Calculate to update the results.

How the calculation works

The calculator totals entered launch costs, adds a contingency percentage and then includes monthly overhead for the selected runway period.

Important limitations
