What this calculator estimates

Medicare Advantage Out-of-Pocket Calculator

Inputs and units

Use the units shown for Monthly plan premium excluding Part B ($), Expected annual covered medical charges ($), Average member cost share (%), Expected annual copays ($), Plan in-network out-of-pocket maximum ($). Required fields cannot be blank. Use zero only where that field and the model allow it. Select Calculate after changing a scenario.

Method and assumptions

The covered medical estimate is annual allowed charges times the assumed member percentage plus entered copays, capped at the entered in-network medical maximum. Add twelve monthly plan premiums. A maximum of zero produces zero covered medical cost.

Worked example

A 35 monthly plan premium and a zero covered-medical maximum produce 420 annual plan cost in this simplified scenario.

Reading the result

  • The headline is the modeled annual total: $3,720.00 on the starting inputs, member medical sharing plus twelve months of the entered premium.
  • The medical line applies the entered cost share to the entered charges and adds copays: $3,300.00, under the entered maximum.
  • The premium line adds twelve months of the entered monthly premium: $420.00 on the starting inputs.
  • The last line shows any sharing above the entered maximum that the model leaves out of the total: $0.00 on the starting inputs.
  • All figures are entered assumptions; actual cost sharing, premiums, and maximums come from the plan's documents and current Medicare rules.

Limitations

Part B premiums, Part D drug expenses, non-covered services and out-of-network costs are outside this estimate. Avoid entering copays already included in the average percentage. A plan's actual service-specific costs and annual limit control.

Common mistakes

  • Including the Part B premium in the plan premium field; the field excludes it, as the label says.
  • Entering a zero maximum to ignore it; the model caps sharing at the entered maximum, so the entered figure matters.
  • Applying the cost share to copays; the model adds copays after the percentage share.
  • Reading the total as a worst case; it uses the entered charges, not the maximum possible.

Frequently asked questions

How is the medical line computed?

The entered charges times the entered share, plus the entered copays, capped at the entered maximum: $3,300.00 on the starting inputs.

What is in the annual total?

The capped medical line plus twelve months of the entered premium: $3,300.00 + $420.00 = $3,720.00 on the starting inputs.

Where do real plan figures come from?

From the plan's documents and current Medicare rules; this page models only the entered numbers.
