What this calculator estimates

Estimate monthly principal, interest, taxes, insurance and HOA for a large home loan.

How to use it

Enter Loan amount ($); Interest rate (%); Loan term; Annual property tax ($); Annual insurance ($); Monthly HOA ($). Select Calculate to update the results.

How the calculation works

Calculate principal and interest with the fixed-payment monthly amortization formula. Add annual property tax/12, annual insurance/12 and the entered monthly HOA charge. The result is the modeled monthly housing payment, with each component shown separately.

Important limitations

The tool does not check current conforming-loan limits or lender qualification rules. Taxes, insurance and HOA costs are held constant, and closing costs, mortgage insurance, variable rates and maintenance are excluded.
