What this calculator estimates

Compare the net cost of leasing a vehicle with buying and retaining equity.

How to use it

Enter Lease upfront cost ($); Lease monthly payment ($); Comparison months; Lease-end fees ($); Buy down payment and fees ($); Buy monthly payment ($); Estimated vehicle equity at end ($). Select Calculate to update the results.

How the calculation works

Lease cost adds upfront, monthly and lease-end charges. Buy cost adds down payment and payments, then subtracts estimated ending equity.
