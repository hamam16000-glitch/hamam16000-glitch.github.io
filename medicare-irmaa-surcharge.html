What this calculator estimates

Medicare IRMAA Surcharge Calculator

Inputs and units

Use the units shown for Monthly Part B IRMAA per person ($), Monthly Part D IRMAA per person ($), People subject to IRMAA, Months charged, Expected monthly reduction after appeal ($). Required fields cannot be blank. Use zero only where that field and the model allow it. Select Calculate after changing a scenario.

Method and assumptions

Add the monthly Part B and Part D surcharges per person and multiply by people. Subtract the entered monthly household appeal reduction, flooring at zero, then multiply by months. The Part B and Part D component totals are before that reduction.

Worked example

Illustrative matching 2026 surcharges of 202.90 and 37.50 for two people over twelve months total 5,769.60 before an appeal reduction.

Limitations

This form does not select a bracket from income or filing status. Enter surcharge amounts from the applicable SSA/CMS notice, excluding the standard Part B and drug-plan premiums. An anticipated appeal is an assumption, not an approved adjustment.
