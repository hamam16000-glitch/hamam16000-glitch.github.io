What this calculator estimates

Estimate a motorcycle loan payment, amount financed and total interest.

Inputs and units

Fill in each field, then select Calculate to update the results. Required fields cannot be blank.

  • Motorcycle purchase price ($): the agreed price before tax and fees. It can be 0 but the financed amount must stay positive.
  • Down payment or cash contribution ($): cash you put in up front. It cannot be negative.
  • Sales tax or financed assessment (%): the tax percentage applied to the price and financed. It can be 0.
  • Financed fees and closing costs ($): fees rolled into the loan. It can be 0.
  • Annual interest rate (%): the yearly rate on the financed amount. It can be 0.
  • Loan term (years): the repayment period in years. It must be above 0.

Method and assumptions

The calculator adds tax on the price and the financed fees, subtracts the down payment for the amount financed, and amortizes that over the term in months. It reports the monthly payment, the amount financed, the total interest, the total paid, and the cash up front.

With the illustrative starting inputs: tax is 7% of 18,000, which is $1,260.00, so the financed amount is 18,000 plus $1,260.00 plus 850 minus 3,000, which is $17,110.00. Over 60 monthly payments at 9.25% the payment is $357.26, the stream totals $21,435.30, and interest is $4,325.30.

Reading the result

  • The monthly payment is the headline: $357.26 on the starting inputs for the financed $17,110.00.
  • The total paid ($21,435.30) against the financed amount shows the $4,325.30 that financing adds over five years.
  • The cash line ($3,000.00) is the down payment only; gear, registration or other costs are outside the loan unless added to the financed fees.

Common mistakes

  • Entering the term in months; the field takes years.
  • Leaving the tax field at a rate that does not apply; the model finances the entered percentage, $1,260.00 on the starting inputs.
  • Comparing only the payment across offers; two offers can share a payment while differing on total cost, which the total line exposes.
  • Reading the payment as a lender quote; the model amortizes your figures at one flat rate, so compare against actual lender offers.

Frequently asked questions

What is included in the financed amount?

Price plus tax and fees, minus the down payment: 18,000 plus $1,260.00 plus 850 minus 3,000 gives $17,110.00 on the starting inputs.

How much does financing add?

$4,325.30 of interest on the starting inputs, taking the $17,110.00 financed to $21,435.30 paid over the 60 payments.

Can I model a larger down payment?

Yes. The down payment comes off before amortization, so every added dollar lowers the payment and the total interest directly.

Important limitations

Actual rates, taxes, dealer charges, closing costs, insurance, collateral rules, balloon terms and lender underwriting vary. This is not a loan offer.

Read the financed amount before comparing payments

Keep accessories and dealer charges separate from the vehicle price unless they are already included. An advertised monthly payment may use a different down payment, term or final balloon than this model.

The model applies the entered percentage tax to the price, adds financed fees and subtracts the cash contribution. A negative financed amount is floored at zero. It rounds years × 12 to a whole number of monthly payments, with payments at each month end. Total loan payments exclude the down payment; total interest is those payments minus the financed principal.

At zero interest, $12,000 financed for 24 months costs $500 per month and $0 interest. A lower payment from extending the term is different from a lower total borrowing cost. Compare the loan offer’s amount financed, interest rate, APR and term as separate figures. CFPB’s loan comparison guidance explains the distinction.
