What this calculator estimates

Estimate Etsy listing, transaction, payment-processing and optional advertising fees with net proceeds and profit per order.

How to use it

Enter Item price ($); Shipping charged ($); Product and packaging cost ($); Actual shipping cost ($); Transaction fee (%); Payment processing rate (%); Payment fixed fee ($); Listing fee ($); Advertising fee (%). Select Calculate to update the results.

How the calculation works

Percentage fees are applied to the entered order amount, then fixed processing and listing fees are added. Product and shipping costs are subtracted for the profit estimate.

Important limitations
