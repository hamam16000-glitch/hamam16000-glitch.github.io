What this calculator estimates

Perform the four basic arithmetic operations and compare the results instantly.

Inputs and units

Fill in each field, then select Calculate to update the results. Required fields cannot be blank.

  • First number: the starting value for the sum, difference, product and quotient. It is also the dividend in the division.
  • Second number: the value added, subtracted, multiplied by, and divided into the first. Entering zero makes the quotient line show Undefined, as the limitations note explains.

Method and assumptions

The calculator adds, subtracts, multiplies and divides the two entered numbers and shows all four results at once. Division by zero is reported as Undefined instead of a number.

With the starting inputs 125 and 25: the sum is 150, the difference is 100, the product is 3,125 and the quotient is 5.

Reading the result

  • Sum is the headline result; the three other lines let you compare the operations side by side.
  • Difference is first minus second in that order; swapping the inputs changes its sign.
  • Quotient is first divided by second; a clean integer like 5 means the second number divides the first exactly.

Common mistakes

  • Entering the numbers in the wrong order for subtraction or division; order changes those two results.
  • Dividing with zero as the second number and expecting a number; the calculator shows Undefined, which the limitations note flags.
  • Reading the displayed decimals as exact when the limitations note says they may be rounded for readability.
  • Using it for chained calculations; each calculation uses only the two fields, not a previous result.

Frequently asked questions

Why does the quotient say Undefined?

The second number is zero. Division by zero has no defined value, so the calculator says so instead of inventing a result.

Can I use decimals or negative numbers?

Yes. Both fields accept any finite numbers; all four operations work the same way with them.

Does it follow order of operations?

There is nothing to order: each line is one operation between the two numbers. For multi-step expressions, compute one step, then re-enter the result.

Important limitations

Division by zero is undefined. Displayed decimals may be rounded for readability.

Order matters for subtraction and division

For first number 8 and second number 2, the form reports sum 10, difference 6, product 16 and quotient 4. Swapping the inputs leaves the sum and product unchanged but changes the difference to −6 and quotient to 0.25. A zero second number makes division undefined; the other three operations can still be calculated.
