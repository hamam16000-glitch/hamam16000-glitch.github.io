What this calculator estimates

Estimate garage-door replacement cost from door count, equipment, installation, openers, disposal, trim, permits and incentives.

Inputs and units

Fill in each field, then select Calculate to update the results. Required fields cannot be blank.

  • Number of garage doors: how many doors are being replaced.
  • Door equipment cost each ($): the price of one door unit. Size, insulation and windows move it, as the limitations note says.
  • Installation labor each ($): the per-door installation labor.
  • Opener and controls each ($): the per-door price for the opener and controls, if replaced. Enter zero to keep existing openers.
  • Old door removal and disposal ($): the fixed total for removing and disposing of the old doors.
  • Framing, trim and finish repairs ($): the fixed amount for framing, trim and finish repairs around the openings.
  • Permits and inspections ($): the fixed permit total.
  • Rebates or discounts ($): the confirmed discount or rebate amount from the quote. The model subtracts it and floors the total at zero.

Method and assumptions

The model prices equipment, labor and openers per door, adds disposal, trim and permit amounts as fixed costs, then subtracts the confirmed discounts, flooring the result at zero.

With the starting inputs: 2 doors at $2,200 give $4,400.00 in equipment, 2 at $650 give $1,300.00 in labor, openers, disposal, trim and permits add $2,400.00, and subtracting $300.00 in discounts gives a total of $7,800.00.

Reading the result

  • Door equipment and Installation labor scale with the door count; the fixed fields do not.
  • Openers, disposal and repairs combines per-door openers with fixed disposal, trim and permit amounts.
  • Discounts applied subtracts at the end; confirm the discount is real and written before entering it.

Common mistakes

  • Entering per-door prices for equipment but a project total for labor, mixing bases.
  • Leaving openers at the default when keeping the existing ones.
  • Counting old-door disposal inside a bundled quote and again in the disposal field.
  • Entering an advertised discount not confirmed in the written quote.

Frequently asked questions

Where does track replacement go?

If the quote prices tracks with the door, they are already in the equipment price. If separate, add the amount to the framing and trim field so it is counted exactly once. The limitations note lists tracks as a pricing factor.

Why floor the total at zero?

So a discount larger than the entered costs cannot produce a negative price. A zero result means the entered discounts exceed the entered project cost.

How do I compare insulated versus standard doors?

Run the calculator once per door type with each one's equipment price; everything else usually stays the same.

Important limitations

Door size, insulation, windows, wind rating, tracks, framing, smart controls and regional labor pricing affect actual quotes.
