What this calculator estimates

Estimate eBay final-value, promoted-listing and per-order fees with net proceeds and order profit.

How to use it

Enter Sale price ($); Shipping charged to buyer ($); Item acquisition cost ($); Actual shipping cost ($); Final value fee (%); Per-order fee ($); Promoted listing fee (%). Select Calculate to update the results.

How the calculation works

The entered final-value rate is applied to the order total and the promoted-listing rate to the sale price. Fixed fees and costs are then included.

Important limitations

eBay fees depend on category, store subscription, seller performance, country, taxes, promoted listing type, refunds and other adjustments.
