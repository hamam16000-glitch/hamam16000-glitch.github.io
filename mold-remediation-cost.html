What this calculator estimates

Estimate mold remediation cost from affected area, testing, containment, repairs, temporary lodging and contingency.

Inputs and units

Fill in each field, then select Calculate to update the results. Required fields cannot be blank.

  • Affected area (sq ft): the area a professional assessment identifies as affected. Do not guess from what is visible; hidden growth is exactly what the limitations note warns about.
  • Remediation cost per sq ft ($): the per-foot remediation rate from a written quote for your situation.
  • Inspection and testing ($): the fixed amount for inspection and any laboratory testing, only when quoted separately.
  • Containment and air treatment ($): the fixed amount for containment and air filtration during the work.
  • Drywall, flooring and finish repairs ($): the fixed amount to rebuild and refinish after remediation. This often rivals the remediation itself.
  • Temporary lodging and other costs ($): the fixed amount for lodging or other temporary costs if the home is unlivable during work. Enter zero if you stay put.
  • Contingency (%): a percentage buffer on the subtotal for growth found after opening surfaces.

Method and assumptions

The model multiplies the affected area by the remediation rate, adds testing, containment, repair and lodging amounts as fixed costs, then applies contingency.

With the starting inputs: 350 square feet at $18 gives $6,300.00 for remediation, testing and containment add $2,450.00, repairs and temporary costs add $4,300.00, and the $13,050 subtotal at 12 percent contingency adds $1,566.00, for a total of $14,616.00.

Reading the result

  • Area-based remediation is the line the assessed area moves; an assessment that finds more area moves it directly.
  • Repairs and temporary costs is often the sleeper line; rebuilding after removal can cost as much as the removal.
  • Contingency at 12 percent reflects that the true scope is only known after surfaces are opened.

Common mistakes

  • Measuring only visible growth when the assessment recommends opening walls; the area drives the largest line.
  • Forgetting the rebuild; remediation removes material, and the repair field is where putting it back is priced.
  • Counting testing inside a bundled quote and again in the testing field.
  • Treating the estimate as a scope decision. Scope comes from a professional assessment; this page only prices the scope you enter.

Frequently asked questions

How do I know the affected area?

From a professional inspection, not from this page. The model prices whatever area you enter; it cannot tell you how much of your home is affected.

Does insurance cover this?

That depends on your policy and the cause. This page only estimates the cost of the scope you enter; your insurer or policy documents are the only source for coverage answers.

Where does fixing the moisture source go?

There is no separate field. Add the quoted amount to the repair field, and note the limitations line: unaddressed source moisture changes the scope itself.

Important limitations

Hidden growth, source moisture, hazardous materials, HVAC contamination, structural damage and local protocols can materially change scope and price.
