What this calculator estimates

Estimate owner-operator trucking profit after fuel, variable and fixed costs.

How to use it

Enter Annual loaded and paid miles; Revenue per mile ($); Truck fuel economy (MPG); Diesel price per gallon ($); Other variable cost per mile ($); Annual fixed costs ($). Select Calculate to update the results.

How the calculation works

Revenue is miles times revenue per mile. Costs include fuel, other variable cost per mile and annual fixed costs.

Important limitations
