What this calculator estimates

Calculate customer lifetime value, acquisition cost ratio, gross-margin payback period and maximum CAC for a target ratio.

How to use it

Enter Monthly revenue per customer ($); Gross margin (%); Monthly customer churn (%); Customer acquisition cost ($); Target LTV to CAC ratio. Select Calculate to update the results.

How the calculation works

Gross profit per customer is monthly revenue multiplied by gross margin. A simple churn-based lifetime value divides monthly gross profit by monthly churn, then compares LTV with CAC.

Important limitations

This simplified formula assumes stable churn and margin. Cohort retention, expansion, discounting, onboarding, support costs and channel mix can change true unit economics.
