What this calculator estimates

Add hours and minutes to a starting time and see the resulting clock time and day shift.

How to use it

Enter Starting hour (0–23); Starting minute (0–59); Hours to add; Minutes to add. Select Calculate to update the results.

How the calculation works

The starting time and added duration are converted to minutes, combined, then normalized to a 24-hour clock.

Important limitations

The tool uses a 24-hour clock and does not account for time zones or daylight-saving changes.
