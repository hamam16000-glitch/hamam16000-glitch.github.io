What this calculator estimates

Estimate a term life insurance premium from coverage, a rate per $1,000, policy fees and the selected term length.

Inputs and units

Fill in each field, then select Calculate to update the results. Required fields cannot be blank.

  • Coverage amount ($): the death benefit the policy would pay. It must be greater than zero.
  • Annual rate per $1,000 of coverage ($): the yearly premium per thousand dollars of coverage.
  • Annual policy fee ($): a flat yearly fee added to the rated premium.
  • Policy term (years): how long the level term runs; the model multiplies the level annual premium by it. It must be at least 1.

Method and assumptions

The calculator applies the rate per $1,000 of coverage, adds the flat policy fee for the annual premium, and projects the total paid over the term assuming the premium stays level. It also shows the monthly equivalent and the annual cost per 100,000 of coverage.

With the illustrative starting inputs: 500,000 over 1,000 times 0.65 is 325, and adding the 75 fee gives an annual premium of $400.00, about $33.33 per month. Over a 20-year level term the total paid is $8,000.00, and the annual cost per 100,000 of coverage is $80.00.

Reading the result

  • Estimated monthly premium is the headline; Estimated annual premium sits beside it.
  • Total premiums over term multiplies the level annual premium by the years; it assumes the premium never changes, which is how level term works but also means it is simple multiplication, not a projection of paid-up value.
  • The limitations note is direct: actual premiums depend on age, health, underwriting, tobacco use and carrier pricing, and this calculator is not an insurance quote.

Common mistakes

  • Treating the estimate as a real quote; the limitations note says premiums depend on age, health, underwriting, tobacco use, occupation, state, riders and carrier pricing, none of which this page asks.
  • Entering a monthly rate; the rate here is annual per $1,000 of coverage.
  • Reading the term total as the policy's value; it is what you pay, not what the policy pays, which is the coverage amount.
  • Assuming the rate scales linearly with coverage in reality; the per-100,000 line is arithmetic on your inputs, and actual rate tiers come from carriers.

Frequently asked questions

Is this an actual life-insurance quote?

No. The limitations note says actual premiums depend on age, health, underwriting, tobacco use, occupation, state, riders and carrier pricing, and that this calculator is not an insurance quote. A licensed professional or carrier provides real figures.

Why show cost per 100,000 of coverage?

It normalizes the premium so you can compare scenarios at different coverage amounts; here the 400 annual premium over 500,000 of coverage is $80.00 per 100,000.

Does the term total account for inflation or returns?

No; it is the level annual premium times the years, a plain sum of what you would pay over the term.

Important limitations

Actual premiums depend on age, health, underwriting, tobacco use, occupation, state, riders and carrier pricing. This calculator is not an insurance quote.
