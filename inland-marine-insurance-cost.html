What this calculator estimates

Estimate an annual inland-marine insurance premium for mobile equipment, tools and property in transit.

How to use it

Enter Insured mobile property value ($); Base annual rate (%); Equipment and transit risk factor; Deductible discount (%); Scheduled-item and policy fees ($). Select Calculate to update the results.

How the calculation works

The estimate applies the entered annual rate to insured property value, adjusts for equipment and transit risk and deductible, then adds endorsements.

Important limitations

Coverage forms, scheduled items, theft controls, job-site exposure, radius, storage, limits, deductibles and claims history can change actual premiums.
