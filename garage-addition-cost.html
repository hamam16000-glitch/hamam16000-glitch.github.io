What this calculator estimates

Estimate a new attached or detached garage addition by floor area. It runs locally in the browser and is intended for planning and comparison.

How to use it

Enter the requested values, select Calculate, review the headline result and supporting figures, then change one input at a time to compare scenarios.

How the calculation works

The calculator multiplies the project quantity by the installed cost per unit, adds the fixed project costs, then applies the contingency allowance and any rebates, insurance payments or discounts.

  • Quantity subtotal = project quantity × installed cost per unit.
  • Base cost = quantity subtotal + fixed project costs.
  • Contingency allowance = base cost × contingency percentage.
  • Estimated total = base cost + contingency allowance − rebates, insurance payments or discounts.
  • Average cost per unit = estimated total ÷ project quantity.

Example: For a 600-square-foot garage at $95 per square foot, plus $18,000 for the foundation, utilities and permits, the base cost is $75,000. A 15% contingency adds $11,250, producing an estimated total of $86,250, or $143.75 per square foot.

Important limitations

Actual bids can vary because of material specifications, labor rates, site access, permits, code requirements, disposal, contractor scope and unexpected conditions discovered after work begins.

This estimate does not automatically include work outside the entered quantity and fixed costs. Add separate allowances for related repairs, restoration, engineering or hazardous-material work when relevant.

Frequently asked questions

Is this result exact?

No. It is an adjustable planning estimate based on the values entered and the method described above.

Can I enter Arabic or Persian digits?

Yes. Numeric fields normalize Arabic and Persian digits to English digits.

Does the calculator store my inputs?

No. The calculation runs locally in your browser and does not require an account.
