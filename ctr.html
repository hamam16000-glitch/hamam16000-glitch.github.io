Quick summary: Calculate click-through rate from impressions and clicks, then estimate clicks and spend for another impression level.

Formula, worked scenarios and limitations

Match clicks to the impressions that produced them

Enter observed impressions and clicks for the same campaign, audience and time period. CTR = clicks ÷ impressions × 100. The future-impression field is a separate scenario; its click estimate assumes the observed rate stays constant.

The average-cost-per-click field is supplied by you. It is multiplied by projected clicks to illustrate spend; the form does not request or retrieve an auction price.

Read the starting example in complete units

With 250,000 impressions and 3,750 clicks, CTR is 1.5%, equivalent to 15 clicks per 1,000 impressions. Applying that rate to 400,000 future impressions gives 6,000 projected clicks.

At the entered CPC of 2.40, those projected clicks imply spend of 14,400. The approximately 66.7 impressions per click figure is the inverse of the observed click fraction, not a promise that every 67th impression will be clicked.

Use downstream events to interpret the rate

If 6,000 projected clicks were followed by a 2% purchase rate, they would imply 120 purchases under that extra assumption. This form does not accept a purchase rate, so that outcome is not part of its result.

A higher click-through rate can accompany less relevant traffic or a different placement. Compare campaigns with the same event definition and inspect actual conversions, value and cost separately in conversion rate and CPA.

Counting and projection limits

A click and an impression must refer to compatible reporting definitions. Some platform interaction metrics can include more than one action associated with an impression; do not mix those metrics with a one-click-event interpretation.

The calculation does not assess viewability, invalid activity, statistical significance, conversion quality or profitability. Changing future impression volume alone does not prove that the historical rate or entered CPC will remain attainable.
