What this calculator estimates

Estimate net rideshare earnings after platform fees and vehicle costs.

Inputs and units

Fill in each field, then select Calculate to update the results. Required fields cannot be blank.

  • Gross passenger fares and tips ($): the total fares and tips for the period before the platform's share. It can be 0.
  • Platform fee share (%): the platform's percentage of the gross. It can be 0 and cannot exceed 100.
  • Miles driven: the total miles driven for the work in the period. It can be 0.
  • Vehicle cost per mile ($): your estimate of fuel and wear per mile. It can be 0.
  • Hours worked: the hours worked in the period. It must be above 0.

Method and assumptions

The calculator takes the platform's percentage out of the gross, subtracts the miles times the per-mile vehicle cost, and reports the net, the net per hour, the platform fee, and the vehicle cost.

With the illustrative starting inputs: the platform fee is 22% of 1,800, which is $396.00, the 950 miles at 0.4 a mile cost $380.00, so the net is $1,024.00, which is $22.76 per hour over 45 hours.

Reading the result

  • The headline is the net: $1,024.00 on the starting inputs, what the gross leaves after the platform share and the vehicle cost.
  • The per-hour line ($22.76 / hour) is the comparable figure across weeks; it falls when the same net spreads over more hours.
  • The fee ($396.00) and vehicle ($380.00) lines show the two drains separately; on these figures they are nearly equal, so shaving either one pays about the same.

Common mistakes

  • Entering driver earnings instead of the gross; the field is fares and tips before the platform's share, and a net figure removes the fee twice.
  • Using the vehicle's fuel-only cost; the per-mile field is your own estimate of fuel plus wear, and understating it overstates the net.
  • Counting only trip hours; the model divides by the hours you enter, so include waiting time if you want the net per hour actually worked.

Frequently asked questions

Is the platform fee a fixed percentage?

The model applies the percentage you enter to the gross, 22% giving $396.00 on the starting inputs; the platform's actual share can vary by trip, so check the platform's own statements for real figures.

Does the vehicle cost use an official rate?

The model uses the per-mile figure you enter as your own estimate of fuel and wear; any tax deduction follows the tax authority's own rules, so confirm those separately.

Can the net go negative?

Yes. If the fee and vehicle cost exceed the gross, the model shows the negative net rather than flooring it.

Important limitations

Taxes, unpaid waiting time, insurance, financing and local platform adjustments are not included unless reflected in the inputs.
