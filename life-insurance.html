What this calculator does

The Life Insurance Calculator estimates a potential coverage amount from income replacement, debts, future obligations, and existing resources represented by the page.

How to use the calculator step by step

Enter income, outstanding debts, major future financial needs, existing savings, and current coverage if requested. Use realistic assumptions rather than simply selecting the largest possible amount.

How the calculation works

A needs-based estimate adds financial obligations and desired income replacement, then subtracts assets or existing coverage available to survivors.

Worked example and scenario testing

A household may need coverage for a mortgage, several years of income replacement, education goals, and final expenses, minus savings or existing insurance already available.

How to interpret your result

The result is a planning estimate of financial need, not an insurance recommendation or guaranteed underwriting amount.

Accuracy, assumptions, and limitations

Actual needs depend on family structure, spouse income, dependents, debts, taxes, inflation, benefits, estate planning, and insurer underwriting rules.

Common mistakes to avoid

Do not rely only on a simple multiple of salary when major debts or dependents make household needs very different. Also avoid counting the same asset twice.

Privacy and browser-based calculations

Use only general financial amounts. Do not enter medical records, policy numbers, Social Security numbers, or insurer credentials.
