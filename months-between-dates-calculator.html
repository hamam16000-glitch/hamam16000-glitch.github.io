What this calculator estimates

Estimate full calendar months, remaining days and total days between two dates.

Inputs and units

Fill in each field, then select Calculate to update the results. Required fields cannot be blank.

  • Start year: the start year, a whole value.
  • Start month: the start month, from 1 to 12.
  • Start day: the start day; the date must exist on the calendar.
  • End year: the end year, a whole value.
  • End month: the end month, from 1 to 12.
  • End day: the end day; the date must exist on the calendar.

Method and assumptions

The calculator counts the whole calendar months between the two dates, anchoring on the start day of month where the end month allows it, then reports the leftover days, the total days, and the span in decimal months using a fixed average month length.

With the illustrative starting inputs: from March 15, 2025 to July 25, 2026 is 16 months with 10 days remaining, the span totals 497 days, and the decimal view is 16.32887739 months.

Worked example

January 31 to February 28, 2026 is 1 completed month and 0 remaining days. January 31 to March 1 is 1 month and 1 day. Reversing the two dates gives the same interval.

Reading the result

  • The headline is the whole-month count: 16 months on the starting inputs, with the leftover shown separately as 10 days rather than folded into a fraction.
  • The days line (497 days) is the raw span, and the decimal line (16.32887739) divides it by the model's fixed average month length, so it will not exactly match the whole-month view.
  • The order does not matter: enter the dates in either direction and the model measures the same span.

Common mistakes

  • Reading the decimal line as the exact month count; it divides the days by an average month length, while the headline counts whole calendar months.
  • Expecting the remaining days to complete a month; they are what is left after the last whole month: 10 days on the starting inputs.
  • Entering a date that does not exist; the form rejects invalid calendar dates.

Frequently asked questions

How are whole months counted?

By calendar month from the start date's anchor day: March 15, 2025 to July 15, 2026 is 16 months, and the remaining 10 days run to July 25.

Why does the decimal differ from the headline?

It divides the 497 days by a fixed average month length, giving 16.32887739, while the headline counts real calendar months.

Does the order of the dates matter?

No. The model swaps them if needed and measures the same span.

Limitations

A decimal month is an average-length month, not an exact calendar month. Contract and legal rules may use different anniversary conventions.
