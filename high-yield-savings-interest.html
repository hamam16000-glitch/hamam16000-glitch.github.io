What this calculator estimates

Project a high-yield savings balance with monthly deposits, APY and account fees.

Inputs and units

Fill in each field, then select Calculate to update the results. Required fields cannot be blank.

  • Starting balance ($): the balance at the start. It can be 0.
  • Monthly deposit ($): the amount added each month. It can be 0.
  • Annual percentage yield (%): the APY the balance earns. It can be 0.
  • Saving period (months): the number of months, a whole number up to 1200.
  • Monthly account fee ($): the monthly fee, charged only up to the available balance. It can be 0.

Method and assumptions

The calculator converts the APY into the equivalent monthly rate, then steps month by month: the balance earns its interest, the deposit is added, and the fee is taken but never more than the available balance. It also shows the total paid in, the interest earned, and the fees charged.

With the illustrative starting inputs: 15,000 plus 750 a month for 24 months at 4.5% grows to $35,161.96. The total paid in is $33,000.00, the interest earned is $2,161.96, and no fees are charged.

Worked example

Starting with 100, no deposits, 0% APY and a 30 monthly fee for twelve months ends at zero. Only 100 in fees can be paid from that balance, and interest earned remains zero.

Reading the result

  • The headline is the ending balance: $35,161.96 on the starting inputs, deposits plus two years of compounding at the entered yield.
  • The interest line ($2,161.96) is what the yield adds over the $33,000.00 paid in; it moves with the APY and the period, both of which are your entries.
  • The fee line ($0.00) confirms the model caps fees at the available balance, so a fee can shrink growth but never drive the balance negative.

Common mistakes

  • Entering a yearly deposit; the deposit field is per month, and the model adds it every month of the period.
  • Entering the period in years; the field is whole months, up to 1200.
  • Reading the ending balance as guaranteed; the entered yield is an assumption, and actual account yields change, so treat the figure as the arithmetic of that assumption.

Frequently asked questions

How does the fee work?

It is charged monthly but capped at the available balance, so on the starting inputs with a 0 fee the line stays $0.00 and the balance can never go negative from fees.

Why is the interest not a simple percentage of the deposits?

Interest compounds monthly on the growing balance at the monthly equivalent of the entered APY, so later months earn more than earlier ones; that is how 33,000 of deposits becomes $35,161.96 over 24 months at 4.5%.

Does the model match my bank's crediting?

The model uses the APY-to-monthly conversion and monthly crediting; an account's own crediting and rate changes follow the bank's terms.

Limitations

Real bank fee policies can include waivers or overdrafts; this model does not. Deposits are assumed at month end. Months must be whole values from 0 to 1,200. APY remains constant and taxes are excluded.

APY already contains annual compounding

The account projection derives a monthly rate from the entered effective APY. Do not divide APY by 12 as though it were a nominal annual rate. Match deposit timing and monthly fees to the period being compared; a fee can consume the interest on a small balance. This is a fixed-APY scenario: a real savings rate can change and the form does not fetch an account’s current terms.
