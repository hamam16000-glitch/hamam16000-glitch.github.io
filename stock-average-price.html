What this calculator estimates

Calculate the weighted average share price after an additional stock purchase.

How to use it

Enter Existing shares; Existing average price per share ($); New shares purchased; New purchase price per share ($); Trading fees ($). Select Calculate to update the results.

How the calculation works

The new average price is total existing and new cost, including fees, divided by total shares.

Important limitations

Tax-lot identification, wash-sale adjustments, return of capital and corporate actions can affect tax basis.

Average price is weighted by shares

Buying 100 shares at $10 and 50 shares at $20 costs $2,000 before fees. The average is $2,000 divided by 150 shares, or about $13.33; averaging the two quoted prices would incorrectly give $15. Fractional shares are valid here. The displayed cost average is not a determination of which tax lot is sold.
