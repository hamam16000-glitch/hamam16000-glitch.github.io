What this calculator estimates

Estimate a wedding-insurance premium from wedding budget, guest count, cancellation coverage, liability, weather options and deductible.

Inputs and units

Fill in each field, then select Calculate to update the results. Required fields cannot be blank.

  • Wedding budget ($): the total wedding budget, the rating base for the cancellation premium.
  • Base cancellation premium rate (%): the percentage of budget charged as the base cancellation premium.
  • Expected guests: the guest count; each guest adds the flat liability charge.
  • Liability charge per guest ($): the flat liability amount added per guest.
  • Additional liability coverage ($): flat cost for extra liability coverage.
  • Weather and vendor add-ons ($): flat add-ons for weather and vendor coverage.
  • Deductible discount (%): a percentage credit off the combined premium, from 0 to 100.

Method and assumptions

The calculator applies the base rate to the budget, adds the per-guest liability charge and the flat add-ons, then applies the deductible discount to the combined figure. It also shows the premium as a percentage of the budget.

With the illustrative starting inputs: the base premium is 50,000 times 1%, which is $500.00, and 150 guests at 1 each add $150.00. With liability at 300 and weather add-ons at 250, add-ons total $550.00, and the 5% discount on the combined 1,200 gives a total premium of $1,140.00, an effective rate of 2.28% of the budget.

Reading the result

  • Estimated total premium is the headline, combining cancellation, liability and add-on coverages after the discount.
  • Base premium, Guest liability and Add-ons show the three parts before the discount.
  • Effective rate restates the total against the budget; 2.28% against the 1% base rate here, on illustrative inputs only. The limitations note says availability, exclusions and purchase timing affect actual premiums.

Common mistakes

  • Applying the discount to the base only; here the 5% comes off the combined base, guest and add-on figure.
  • Entering the per-guest charge as a total; the model multiplies it by the guest count.
  • Reading the effective rate as an insurer's rate; it restates your own inputs, and coverage availability, exclusions and venue requirements shape real premiums, per the limitations note.
  • Assuming cancellation coverage pays for any reason; the limitations note says cancellation causes and purchase timing affect coverage, and the policy defines them.

Frequently asked questions

Is this an actual wedding-insurance quote?

No. Coverage availability, exclusions, cancellation causes, venue requirements, alcohol, destination events, policy limits and purchase timing can affect actual premiums, as the limitations note says; this is a budgeting model.

Why a per-guest liability charge?

Liability exposure scales with the crowd, so the model adds a flat amount per guest on top of the budget-based premium; the charge itself is your editable input.

Does the discount apply to everything?

In this model, yes: the percentage credit applies to the combined base, guest and add-on figure, with no separate flat fees outside it.

Important limitations

Coverage availability, exclusions, cancellation causes, venue requirements, alcohol, destination events, policy limits and purchase timing can affect actual premiums.
