What this calculator estimates

Estimate an annual flood insurance premium from building and contents coverage, editable rates, policy fees and mitigation discounts. It is designed for fast planning and comparison in the United States. The output is an estimate, not a binding quote, approval, tax determination, insurance offer or professional recommendation.

How to use it

Enter the requested values using a single consistent time period and currency. Select Calculate, review the headline result and supporting figures, then change one assumption at a time to compare another scenario.

How the calculation works

Coverage amounts are converted to units of $1,000 and multiplied by the entered rates. Policy fees are added and the mitigation discount is subtracted.

Important limitations

Flood zone, elevation, construction, foundation type, occupancy, claims history and program rules drive real premiums. This is not an NFIP or private-market quote.

Frequently asked questions

Is the result exact?

No. It is a planning estimate based on the assumptions entered.

Can I compare different scenarios?

Yes. Change one input at a time so you can see which assumption changes the result.

Does the calculator store my inputs?

The calculation runs in the browser and does not require an account. Avoid entering unnecessary confidential information.
