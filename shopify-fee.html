What this calculator estimates

Estimate online-store platform and payment fees using adjustable assumptions.

How to use it

Enter Monthly online sales ($); Monthly orders; Monthly platform plan fee ($); Payment percentage fee (%); Per-order fee ($); Apps and other monthly fees ($). Select Calculate to update the results.

How the calculation works

The estimate adds plan, percentage, per-order and other entered fees.

Important limitations

Actual plans, gateways, card types and app charges can change. Enter current contract terms.

The entered rates determine the fee scenario

Payment processing, a platform transaction fee and a subscription are distinct charges. Enter only the rates applicable to the payment arrangement being compared, and allocate recurring charges across the same period as the order count. The displayed defaults are editable examples; this calculator does not retrieve Shopify’s current contract or regional price schedule.
