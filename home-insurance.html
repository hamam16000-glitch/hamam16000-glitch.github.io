What this calculator does

Estimate an annual home-insurance budget from a replacement-cost amount and a planning rate you enter.

How to use the calculator

Enter the dwelling replacement cost, meaning what it would cost to rebuild the home, not its market or purchase price. Then enter a planning rate as a percentage of that cost (type 0.55 for 0.55%), any extra annual endorsements in dollars, and the discount percentage you expect for deductibles or policy discounts.

Select Calculate to see the annual premium after discount, the monthly equivalent, the premium before discounts and the discount value.

How the calculation works

Budget before discount = dwelling replacement cost x planning percentage / 100 + annual endorsements. Discount = that budget x discount percentage / 100. The annual result is the budget minus discount; monthly budget is annual result divided by 12.

Worked example

For a replacement-cost input of 400,000, planning rate 0.55%, annual endorsements 250 and discount 10%, the budget before discount is 2,450. The discount is 245, giving 2,205 annually or 183.75 monthly.

How to interpret the result

The large figure is the estimated annual premium after discount, and the monthly line is that figure divided by 12. The before-discount line is the dwelling cost times the rate, plus endorsements. With the defaults (400,000 at 0.55%, 250 endorsements, 10% discount) it shows $2,450.00 before discount, a $245.00 discount, $2,205.00 a year and $183.75 a month.

The rate has the largest effect. Changing only the rate to 0.80% raises the annual figure to $3,105.00 ($258.75 a month). If you have a quote, you can work backward to a planning rate: subtract endorsements from the annual quote, divide by the dwelling cost and multiply by 100.

Assumptions and limitations

The planning percentage is entered by the user; it is not a current market quote. The form does not rate the property using location, construction, catastrophe risk, claims history or insurer rules. Replacement cost is the rebuilding basis, not automatically the home's purchase price.

Common mistakes

Using the purchase price or land value instead of the rebuilding cost.

Typing the rate as 0.0055 instead of 0.55.

Entering monthly endorsement costs in the annual endorsements field.

Expecting the discount to apply only to the base premium. In this model it applies to the base amount plus endorsements.

Reading the figure as a quote. The planning rate is your own assumption and does not reflect location, claims history or insurer rules.

Privacy

This form needs a rebuilding-cost figure, a planning rate, an endorsement amount and a discount. It does not ask for a property address, policy number or personal details. The calculation runs in your browser.

Frequently asked questions

Does the calculator fetch current premiums?

No. Enter a rate or quote-derived assumption for your own comparison.

Are monthly and annual results separate premiums?

No. The monthly result is one twelfth of the annual budget.

What does the discount apply to?

In this model it applies to the combined dwelling-rate budget and annual endorsements.
