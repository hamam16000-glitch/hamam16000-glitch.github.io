What this calculator estimates

Estimate landscaping project costs for lawn, planting beds, plants, irrigation, hardscape, design and contingency.

Inputs and units

Fill in each field, then select Calculate to update the results. Required fields cannot be blank.

  • Lawn and ground-cover area (sq ft): the area getting lawn or ground cover.
  • Lawn preparation and installation per sq ft ($): the per-foot rate for prep and installation of the lawn.
  • Planting bed area (sq ft): the area of planting beds, measured separately from the lawn.
  • Beds, mulch and soil per sq ft ($): the per-foot rate for bed preparation, mulch and soil.
  • Trees and plants ($): the fixed total for trees and plants. Mature trees cost more, as the limitations note says.
  • Irrigation and drainage ($): the fixed total for irrigation and drainage work.
  • Paths, walls and hardscape ($): the fixed total for hardscape elements.
  • Design and permits ($): the fixed total for design services and permits.
  • Contingency (%): a percentage buffer on the subtotal for soil or grading surprises.

Method and assumptions

The model prices lawn and beds per square foot at their own rates, adds plants, irrigation, hardscape and design as fixed costs, then applies contingency. The last result line combines hardscape, design and contingency.

With the starting inputs: 5,000 square feet at $1.75 gives $8,750.00 for lawn, 800 square feet at $7.50 gives $6,000.00 for beds, plants and irrigation add $14,500.00, hardscape and design add $14,500, and the $43,750 subtotal at 10 percent contingency adds $4,375.00, for a total of $48,125.00.

Reading the result

  • Lawn and ground cover and Planting beds scale with their own areas and rates.
  • Plants and irrigation combines two fixed fields; the plant palette and the irrigation scope both move it.
  • Hardscape, design and contingency bundles the two fixed fields with the contingency, so a big hardscape plan shows up there.

Common mistakes

  • Measuring the whole lot as lawn area when beds, hardscape and the house cover much of it.
  • Leaving hardscape at the default for a lawn-only project.
  • Forgetting irrigation when comparing a bid that includes it against one that does not.
  • Ignoring ongoing maintenance, which the limitations note lists; this is a project cost, not a yearly budget.

Frequently asked questions

Why are hardscape and design grouped with contingency?

That is how the result list groups the fixed fields and the contingency. The arithmetic above shows each amount separately.

How do I phase the project?

Run the calculator once per phase with that phase's areas and fixed costs. Contingency applies per phase, which is reasonable when later phases are less certain.

Where does drainage correction go?

In the irrigation and drainage field. The limitations note lists drainage and grading as bid factors, so use the quoted amount.

Important limitations

Grading, drainage, mature trees, site access, soil, climate, ongoing maintenance and local labor rates can change actual landscaping bids.
