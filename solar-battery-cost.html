What this calculator estimates

Estimate a residential solar-battery project cost after equipment, installation, electrical upgrades, permits and incentives. It is designed for fast planning and comparison in the United States. The output is an estimate, not a binding quote, approval, tax determination, insurance offer, legal conclusion, medical recommendation or professional advice.

How to use it

Enter the requested values using a single consistent time period and currency. Select Calculate, review the headline result and supporting figures, then change one assumption at a time to compare another scenario.

How the calculation works

Battery equipment cost is multiplied by the number of units, then installation, electrical and permit costs are added. Entered incentives are subtracted.

Important limitations

Battery capacity, chemistry, inverter compatibility, backup loads, utility programs, tax eligibility, labor and electrical conditions vary. Verify current incentives independently.

Frequently asked questions

Is the result exact?

No. It is a planning estimate based on the assumptions entered.

Can I compare different scenarios?

Yes. Change one input at a time so you can see which assumption changes the result.

Does the calculator store my inputs?

The calculation runs in the browser and does not require an account. Avoid entering unnecessary confidential information.
