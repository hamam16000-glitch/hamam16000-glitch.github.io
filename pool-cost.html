What this calculator estimates

Estimate an in-ground swimming pool project budget including shell, decking, equipment, site work, fencing, permits and contingency.

Inputs and units

Fill in each field, then select Calculate to update the results. Required fields cannot be blank.

  • Pool surface area (sq ft): the pool's water surface area. The shell is priced per square foot of this area.
  • Pool shell and finish per sq ft ($): the per-foot rate for the shell and interior finish, for the pool type you plan. Pool type is a major cost driver, as the limitations note says.
  • Decking area (sq ft): the surrounding deck area, measured separately from the pool.
  • Decking cost per sq ft ($): the per-foot rate for the decking material you plan.
  • Pump, filter, heater and equipment ($): the fixed total for the equipment set.
  • Excavation and site work ($): the fixed amount for excavation and site work. Soil, access and groundwater move this, per the limitations note.
  • Safety fence, permits and inspections ($): the fixed total for the safety barrier, permits and inspections. Local safety rules determine what is required; get that from your local requirements and include the quoted cost.
  • Contingency (%): a percentage buffer on the subtotal for soil and groundwater surprises.

Method and assumptions

The model prices the shell per square foot of pool area and the deck per square foot of deck area, adds equipment, site work and fence or permit amounts as fixed costs, then applies contingency. The last result line combines the fence and permit amount with the contingency.

With the starting inputs: 450 square feet at $115 gives $51,750.00 for the shell, 700 square feet at $18 gives $12,600.00 for decking, equipment and site work add $28,000.00, the fence and permits add $9,000, and the $101,350 subtotal at 12 percent contingency adds $12,162.00, for a total of $113,512.00.

Reading the result

  • Pool shell and finish is the dominant line and moves with area, rate and pool type.
  • Fence, permits and contingency is a combined line: the fixed fence and permit amount plus the whole contingency.
  • Equipment and site work combines two fixed fields; site conditions move it as much as equipment choices do.

Common mistakes

  • Measuring the deck as part of the pool area or the reverse; they carry different rates.
  • Treating the safety barrier as optional; local safety rules apply, as the limitations note says, so include the quoted cost.
  • Leaving site work at the default when access or groundwater complicates the dig.
  • Comparing the total to an advertised shell-only price.

Frequently asked questions

Why is contingency combined with the fence line?

That is how the result list groups it: the fence and permit amount plus the contingency on the whole subtotal. The individual amounts are still visible in the arithmetic above.

Where does landscaping restoration go?

There is no separate field; the limitations note lists landscaping. Add the quoted amount to the excavation and site work field as a fixed cost.

How do I compare pool types?

Run the calculator once per type with each type's shell rate and equipment figure. The arithmetic is the same; the inputs carry the difference.

Important limitations

Soil, access, groundwater, utility relocation, pool type, finish, automation, landscaping and local safety rules can significantly change costs.
