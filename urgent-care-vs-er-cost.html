What this calculator estimates

Compare estimated out-of-pocket costs for urgent care and an emergency room.

How to use it

Enter Urgent care allowed charge ($); Emergency room allowed charge ($); Deductible remaining ($); Coinsurance after deductible (%); Urgent care copay ($); ER copay ($). Select Calculate to update the results.

How the calculation works

Each setting applies the remaining deductible, coinsurance and setting-specific copay to the entered allowed charge.

Important limitations

Medical urgency should determine where care is sought. Call emergency services for severe or life-threatening symptoms; cost should not delay emergency care.

This comparison cannot choose a care setting

The two amounts are financial scenarios using the allowed charges and cost-sharing assumptions entered. They do not assess symptoms, urgency or medical suitability. Deductible, coinsurance and an additional copay are combined under the disclosed model; a real plan may use a different arrangement or separate facility and professional claims.
