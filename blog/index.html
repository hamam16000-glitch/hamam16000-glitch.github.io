What makes these guides useful

Each guide is paired with a working calculator, but it does not simply repeat the calculator screen. It explains the inputs, shows a worked example, identifies costs or rules the tool does not include, and points to an authoritative source for facts that can change.

Calculator results are estimates. Use them to compare scenarios, not as a lender quote, investment promise, tax determination, or substitute for professional advice.
