What this calculator estimates

Estimate a business website project cost from pages, design, development, e-commerce, content, SEO, setup and contingency.

Inputs and units

Fill in each field, then select Calculate to update the results. Required fields cannot be blank.

  • Website pages: the number of pages in the site. It must be at least 1.
  • Content and setup per page ($): the content and setup cost for one page.
  • UX and visual design hours: the design hours for the project.
  • Design hourly rate ($): the hourly rate for design work.
  • Development hours: the development hours for the project.
  • Development hourly rate ($): the hourly rate for development work.
  • E-commerce or advanced feature allowance ($): a project allowance for e-commerce or advanced features.
  • SEO, copy and launch preparation ($): search, copy and launch work as a project total.
  • Hosting, analytics and technical setup ($): one-time hosting, analytics and technical setup.
  • Contingency (%): an allowance for unknowns, as a percentage of the subtotal.

Method and assumptions

The calculator multiplies the page count by the per-page content cost, adds the design and development hours at their rates as the labor line, adds the feature, SEO and setup allowances, applies the contingency percentage to the subtotal, and reports the total plus an average cost per page.

With the illustrative starting inputs: content is 20 times 250, which is $5,000.00, labor is 80 times 100 plus 160 times 120, which is $27,200.00, and the feature allowances add to $8,500.00. The subtotal is $40,700.00, the total with 10% contingency is $44,770.00, and the average is $44,770.00 over 20, which is $2,238.50 per page.

Reading the result

  • The project total is the headline: $44,770.00 on the starting inputs.
  • Labor ($27,200.00) is the largest line here, combining the design and development hours at their rates.
  • The $2,238.50 per-page average spreads the flat feature allowances across the pages, so the page count moves it even when the allowances stay fixed.

Common mistakes

  • Entering a lump-sum content quote in the per-page field; the model multiplies it by the page count.
  • Combining design and development into one hours field; the two lines take different rates.
  • Reading the per-page average as the cost of one more page; it averages the flat allowances across pages, so it is not a marginal cost.
  • Setting the feature allowance to zero when the site needs e-commerce; the model only adds what you enter.

Frequently asked questions

Is ongoing hosting included?

No. The hosting field here is one-time setup; recurring hosting fees are outside the model, as the limitations note says.

What does the contingency apply to?

The whole subtotal: 10% on $40,700.00 on the starting inputs.

Can I price a single landing page?

Enter 1 page and trim the feature allowances to what applies; they add in full otherwise.

Important limitations

Scope, integrations, content quality, accessibility, custom design, e-commerce, migration, security, maintenance and vendor location can change actual cost.
