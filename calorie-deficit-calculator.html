What this calculator estimates

Calorie Deficit Calculator

Inputs and units

Use the units shown for Estimated maintenance calories per day, Planned daily calorie intake, Days following the plan each week, Static energy conversion assumption (calories per pound). Required fields cannot be blank. Use zero only where that field and the model allow it. Select Calculate after changing a scenario.

Method and assumptions

Daily energy difference is estimated maintenance calories minus entered intake. Multiply by selected days per week. Dividing that weekly difference by the entered calories-per-pound assumption gives a static energy equivalent; multiply it by four for a four-week equivalent.

Worked example

A 500-calorie daily difference for seven days gives 3,500 calories. At an assumed 3,500 calories per pound, that is one pound of energy equivalent, not a promise of one pound of weight loss.

Reading the result

  • The headline is the daily gap between the entered maintenance and intake: 500 calories/day on the starting inputs.
  • The weekly line multiplies the daily gap by the entered days per week: 3500 calories on the starting inputs.
  • The weight lines divide the weekly gap by the entered calories-per-pound figure: 1 lb/week on the starting inputs, and 4 lb when the weekly figure is multiplied by 4.
  • A negative gap means the entered intake exceeds maintenance; the model reports the signed result, not a judgment about any person's plan.

Limitations

Body-weight changes are not linear conversions of calorie deficits: metabolism adapts, and water and body composition vary. This tool does not prescribe calorie intake or forecast physiological weight loss. A negative difference is an energy surplus.

Common mistakes

  • Entering a goal intake as maintenance; maintenance is the modeled current need, and swapping the two flips the sign of the gap.
  • Setting days per week below 1 or above 7; the page accepts 1 to 7.
  • Treating the calories-per-pound figure as fixed physiology; it is an entered modeling assumption (starting value 3500), and the result scales with it.
  • Reading the four-week line as a forecast; the model applies the same gap every week with no adaptation.

Frequently asked questions

How is the weekly change estimated?

The daily gap of 500 calories is multiplied by the 7 entered days, then divided by the entered 3500 calories per pound, giving 1 lb/week on the starting inputs.

What if the result is negative?

The entered intake is above the entered maintenance, so the model reports a negative gap; that describes the numbers entered, not a recommended plan.

Where does the calories-per-pound figure come from?

It is an input, not a constant built into the page: the starting value is 3500, and the estimate changes in proportion if it is edited.
