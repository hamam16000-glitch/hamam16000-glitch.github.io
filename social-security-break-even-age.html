What this calculator estimates

Estimate the age when cumulative delayed benefits catch up with earlier claiming. It runs locally in the browser and is intended for planning and comparison.

How to use it

Enter the requested values, select Calculate, review the headline result and supporting figures, then change one input at a time to compare scenarios.

How the calculation works

Forgone early benefits are divided by the monthly increase from delayed claiming.

Important limitations

Taxes, COLAs, survivor benefits, earnings tests and mortality are not fully modeled.

Frequently asked questions

Is this result exact?

No. It is an adjustable planning estimate based on the values entered and the method described above.

Can I enter Arabic or Persian digits?

Yes. Numeric fields normalize Arabic and Persian digits to English digits.

Does the calculator store my inputs?

No. The calculation runs locally in your browser and does not require an account.
