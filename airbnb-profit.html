What this calculator estimates

Estimate monthly short-term rental revenue, operating costs, net profit and annualized return from nightly rate and occupancy assumptions.

How to use it

Enter Average nightly rate ($); Available nights per month; Expected occupancy (%); Average stay length (nights); Cleaning fee charged per stay ($); Platform and payment fees (%); Variable cost per occupied night ($); Cleaning cost per stay ($); Mortgage, rent, utilities and fixed costs ($). Select Calculate to update the results.

How the calculation works

Booked nights are available nights multiplied by occupancy. Nightly and cleaning revenue are reduced by platform fees, variable expenses, cleaning and fixed monthly costs.

Important limitations
