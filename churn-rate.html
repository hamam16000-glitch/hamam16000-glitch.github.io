What this calculator estimates

Calculate customer churn, retention and an approximate customer lifetime.

How to use it

Enter Customers at start of period; Customers lost during period; Period length. Select Calculate to update the results.

How the calculation works

Period customer churn = lost customers / customers present at the beginning. Retention is 1 minus that fraction. For a period of m months, the equivalent constant monthly churn is 1 - (1 - period churn)^(1/m). The illustrative lifetime is 1 / monthly churn in months.

Important limitations

This measures customer-count churn, not revenue churn. The lifetime illustration assumes an unchanging churn probability and is not a prediction of any particular customer's behavior. New acquisitions during the period are not added to the starting cohort. With no observed churn, a finite lifetime is not estimated.

An inferred monthly rate is not a monthly observation

For a multi-month period, the page solves (1 − monthly churn)^months = retained share. This differs from simply dividing total churn by months. Losing 19 of 100 starting customers over two months implies a constant monthly churn rate of 10%, because 0.9 × 0.9 = 0.81. The lifetime illustration assumes this same churn probability continues; it is not a customer-level forecast and does not account for changing cohorts.
