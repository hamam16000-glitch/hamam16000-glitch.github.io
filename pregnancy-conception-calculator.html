What this calculator estimates

Estimate conception date, gestational age and time remaining from an expected due date and reference date.

How to use it

Enter Due-date year; Due-date month; Due-date day; Reference year; Reference month; Reference day. Select Calculate to update the results.

How the calculation works

The estimate subtracts 266 days from the due date for conception and 280 days for the last menstrual period, then compares the reference date.

Reading the result

  • The headline is the estimated conception date: 2026-03-10 on the starting inputs, counted back from the entered due date.
  • The gestation line gives elapsed time from the estimated last menstrual period to the reference date: 21 weeks, 3 days on the starting inputs.
  • The remaining line counts the days from the reference date to the due date: 130 days on the starting inputs.
  • The model applies fixed pregnancy-length conventions, as the method section lists; actual timing differs, and a clinician confirms dating.

Important limitations

Pregnancy dating is an estimate and can differ based on cycle length, ovulation, ultrasound and clinical assessment. Consult a qualified healthcare professional.

Common mistakes

  • Swapping the due-date and reference fields; the reference date is the as-of date for the elapsed and remaining lines.
  • Entering the conception date as the due date; the fields ask for the due date, and the model counts back from it.
  • Reading the weeks figure as measured gestational age; it is computed from entered dates under the model's conventions, not from an examination.
  • Treating the dates as clinical dating; a clinician dates a pregnancy with examination and ultrasound, which the model does not use.

Frequently asked questions

How is the conception date estimated?

The model counts back 266 days from the due date: 2026-03-10 on the starting inputs. The last-menstrual-period line counts back 280 days, to 2026-02-24.

What if the reference date is before the pregnancy start?

The gestation line says so instead of showing weeks; the model reports that the reference date is before the estimated pregnancy.

How precise are these dates?

They follow fixed conventions from the entered due date; real timing varies, and clinicians confirm dating with ultrasound.
