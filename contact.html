Contact & Corrections

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Contact the maintainer

Email hamam16000@gmail.com for calculator errors, accessibility problems, suggestions or general website questions.

What to include in a correction report

  1. The calculator page URL.
  2. The input values, units and selected options.
  3. The result displayed and the result you expected.
  4. The browser or device, if the problem concerns the interface.
  5. A reference or an arithmetic check, when available.

For example: “On the percentage calculator, I selected percentage change, entered original 80 and new 100, and expected 25%.” This makes the report easy to reproduce.

Keep unnecessary personal data out

Do not send passwords, bank details, identity numbers, medical records or account credentials. Use representative values when your original numbers are confidential. Sending email uses your email provider and includes the information you choose to put in the message.

The site’s public source account is hamam16000-glitch on GitHub. A reference to this account identifies the source publisher; it is not a professional accreditation.
