What this calculator estimates

Estimate local or long-distance moving labor, truck and mileage charges.

How to use it

Enter Number of movers; Estimated labor hours; Hourly rate per mover ($); Truck and equipment fee ($); Chargeable distance (miles); Mileage charge ($/mile); Tips and extras ($). Select Calculate to update the results.

How the calculation works

Labor = movers × hours × hourly rate per mover. Mileage = chargeable distance × mileage charge per mile. Add labor, mileage, truck/equipment fees and tips/extras to get the total. The rate is per mover rather than per crew.

Important limitations

Minimum labor hours, stairs, packing, storage, insurance, tolls, travel time and taxes are not added automatically. Include each charge once in the applicable field. This editable arithmetic budget does not establish a carrier's binding estimate.
