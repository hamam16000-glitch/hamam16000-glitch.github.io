What this calculator estimates

Estimate monthly and annual managed IT service cost by users, devices and sites.

Inputs and units

Fill in each field, then select Calculate to update the results. Required fields cannot be blank.

  • Supported users: the people the provider supports. It must be at least 1.
  • Monthly cost per user ($): the monthly support price for each user.
  • Additional managed devices: extra devices under management beyond the users. It cannot be negative.
  • Monthly cost per additional device ($): the monthly management price for each additional device.
  • Office locations: the sites covered. It must be at least 1.
  • Monthly network and site fee ($): the monthly network and site fee for each location.

Method and assumptions

The calculator multiplies users, additional devices and locations by their monthly rates, adds the three lines for the monthly total, and multiplies the monthly total by 12 for the annual line.

With the illustrative starting inputs: users cost 75 times 150, which is $11,250.00 a month, additional devices cost 25 times 40, which is $1,000.00, and sites cost 2 times 750, which is $1,500.00. The monthly total is $13,750.00, and the annual line is $13,750.00 times 12, which is $165,000.00.

Reading the result

  • The monthly total is the headline: $13,750.00 on the starting inputs.
  • The user line ($11,250.00) dominates at the entered rates; devices ($1,000.00) and sites ($1,500.00) are separate lines so each rate stays visible.
  • The annual line ($165,000.00) is the entered month repeated 12 times, with no growth or mid-year change assumed.

Common mistakes

  • Counting servers or shared machines as users; the model prices users and additional devices on separate lines and rates.
  • Entering an annual contract price in a monthly field; every rate field is monthly.
  • Reading the annual line as a contracted price; it is the entered month repeated 12 times.
  • Leaving additional devices at zero when unmanaged machines exist; the model only prices what you enter.

Frequently asked questions

What if there are no extra devices?

Set the additional devices field to 0 and that line drops out of the total.

Does it include one-time onboarding fees?

No. This page models recurring monthly costs only; one-time fees are outside it, as the limitations note says.

What does the per-site fee cover?

Whatever your provider includes in it. Check the provider's own terms and enter the monthly figure they quote.

Important limitations

Projects, onboarding, cybersecurity tools, cloud licenses, after-hours support, hardware and contract minimums may be separate.

Match users, devices and support scope

Confirm whether a monthly service quote is per user, per device or a flat organization-wide fee. Put project work outside the recurring support agreement only in an additional-cost field. A lower calculated total can reflect fewer included services rather than a lower price for the same support coverage.
