Read the complete guide

What this calculator estimates

Estimate total storage-unit cost from monthly rent, rental duration, insurance, move-in fees and expected rate increases. The calculation runs locally in your browser and is intended for planning and comparison.

How to use it

Enter the requested values, select Calculate, review the headline result and supporting figures, then change one input at a time to compare another scenario.

How the calculation works

The estimate totals monthly rent and insurance, adds a simplified mid-period rent-increase allowance and includes move-in fees.

Important limitations

Promotional rates, taxes, access fees, late fees and rate-change timing vary by facility. Review the rental agreement.

Frequently asked questions

Is this result exact?

No. It is a planning estimate based on the values entered and the method described on the page.

Can I enter Arabic or Persian digits?

Yes. Arabic and Persian digits are normalized to English digits inside numeric fields.

Does the calculator store my inputs?

No. The calculation runs locally in your browser and does not require an account.
