Inputs and units

Use the units shown for Current standard monthly Part B premium ($), Full 12-month periods late, Penalty per full year (%), Months premiums are paid, Covered people with the penalty. Required fields cannot be blank. Use zero only where that field and the model allow it. Select Calculate after changing a scenario.

Method and assumptions

Use a 10% premium increase for each full 12-month period late. Round each person's combined standard premium plus penalty to the nearest ten cents. The incremental penalty is that rounded amount less the standard premium. Multiply by people and months. The prefilled standard premium is 202.90 for 2026.

Worked example

Two full late years with the 2026 standard premium produce 243.50 monthly after rounding. The extra 40.60 monthly is 487.20 over twelve months for one person.

Reading the result

  • The headline is the modeled total penalty: $7,308.00 on the starting inputs, over the entered 120 months for 1 person.
  • The monthly penalty line shows the added amount per month per person: $60.90 on the starting inputs.
  • The adjusted premium line shows the entered premium with the penalty applied: $263.80 on the starting inputs.
  • The model applies the entered penalty rate per full year late to the entered premium; the page fixes the rate at 10 and requires whole periods.
  • Penalty rules and premiums follow current official Medicare rules; this page models only the entered figures.

Limitations

This illustration holds the premium constant over the selected months. Actual premiums change by year. Special enrollment periods, qualifying coverage and Medicare Savings Programs may remove a penalty; SSA determines the eligible late periods. IRMAA is separate.

Common mistakes

  • Entering partial years; the model takes full 12-month periods only and rejects fractions.
  • Changing the rate field; the model fixes the penalty rate at 10 per full year and rejects other entries.
  • Entering the penalized premium in the premium field; the field takes the current standard premium before penalty.
  • Reading the total as an official bill; actual penalties follow current Medicare rules and individual records.

Frequently asked questions

How is the penalty computed?

The entered premium is increased by the entered rate for each full period late: 202.90 with 3 periods at 10 gives 263.80 a month, a $60.90 penalty, on the starting inputs.

Why does the rate field reject other values?

The page models the standard 10-per-full-year structure and holds the rate at 10; current official rules govern actual penalties.

Where do real penalty figures come from?

From current official Medicare rules and the person's record; this page models the entered premium, periods, months, and people.
