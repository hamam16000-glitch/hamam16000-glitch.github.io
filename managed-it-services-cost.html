What this calculator estimates

Estimate the first-year cost of managed IT services from users, devices, projects, security services and onboarding.

Inputs and units

Fill in each field, then select Calculate to update the results. Required fields cannot be blank.

  • Supported users: the people the provider supports. It must be above 0.
  • Monthly support cost per user ($): the monthly support price for each user.
  • Managed devices: the devices under management.
  • Monthly device-management cost ($): the monthly management price for each device.
  • Average monthly projects and consulting ($): the monthly average of project and consulting spend.
  • Monthly security stack and monitoring ($): the monthly security and monitoring fee.
  • One-time onboarding and remediation ($): the one-time onboarding and remediation charge.

Method and assumptions

The calculator annualizes the user, device-plus-security and project lines separately, sums them for the recurring annual cost, adds the one-time onboarding for the first-year total, and shows the recurring cost as a monthly average.

With the illustrative starting inputs: users cost 75 times 150 times 12, which is $135,000.00 a year, devices plus security cost 100 times 30 plus 1,000, which is 4,000 a month, or $48,000.00 a year, and projects cost 2,000 times 12, which is $24,000.00. The recurring total is $207,000.00, the first-year total with 8,000 of onboarding is $215,000.00, and the recurring monthly average is $17,250.00.

Reading the result

  • The first-year total is the headline: $215,000.00 on the starting inputs, including the $8,000.00 one-time onboarding.
  • The user line ($135,000.00 a year) is the largest recurring piece; devices and security together add $48,000.00 and projects add $24,000.00.
  • The monthly average ($17,250.00) is the recurring cost divided by 12, so it excludes the one-time onboarding.

Common mistakes

  • Entering annual figures in the monthly fields; every rate field is monthly and the model does the annualizing.
  • Folding onboarding into the projects field; the model then charges it every month instead of once.
  • Counting security tooling in both the device rate and the security field; the model adds both lines.
  • Reading the first-year total as the steady-state cost; the recurring lines alone are $207,000.00 here once onboarding falls away.

Frequently asked questions

What does year two look like?

The recurring lines only: $207,000.00 on the starting inputs, since onboarding is one-time in this model.

Project spend varies month to month. What do I enter?

The monthly average; the model multiplies it by 12 for the annual line.

What does the security fee cover?

Whatever the provider's stack includes. Check the provider's own terms for scope, then enter the monthly figure.

Important limitations

Service scope, response times, locations, device mix, cloud platforms, compliance, projects, after-hours coverage and existing technical debt affect contract pricing.
