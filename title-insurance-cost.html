What this calculator estimates

Estimate owner and lender title-insurance premiums plus endorsements, settlement and recording charges.

How to use it

Enter Property purchase price ($); Mortgage loan amount ($); Owner policy rate per $1,000; Lender policy rate per $1,000; Endorsements and search fees ($); Settlement, recording and administrative fees ($); Simultaneous-policy discount ($). Select Calculate to update the results.

How the calculation works

Owner and lender policy estimates are calculated from editable rates per thousand dollars of purchase price and loan amount. Other charges are added and any entered simultaneous-policy discount is subtracted.

Important limitations
