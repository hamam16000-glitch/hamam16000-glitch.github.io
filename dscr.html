Quick summary: Calculate debt service coverage ratio for a rental or business property.

Formula, worked scenarios and limitations

Match operating income with the same debt period

The debt service coverage ratio compares annual net operating income with annual debt service. Enter income after the operating expenses used in your analysis, but before deducting the same debt service again. Debt service must be positive.

Enter a full year in both fields. For example, annual debt service of 72,000 corresponds to 6,000 per month. Dividing annual income by just one monthly payment would overstate the ratio twelvefold.

The ratio and the remaining amount

DSCR = annual net operating income ÷ annual debt service. Modeled surplus = net operating income − debt service. The ratio has no currency unit as long as both money amounts use the same currency.

Income of 90,000 divided by debt service of 72,000 gives 1.25. The arithmetic surplus after that debt service is 18,000 for the year. A ratio of 1.00 means the two entered totals are equal; it is not a statement about loan approval.

Separate an income stress from a payment stress

Reduce income from 90,000 to 81,000 while holding debt service at 72,000. DSCR becomes 1.125 and surplus becomes 9,000. Alternatively, keep income at 90,000 and raise debt service to 80,000: the same 1.125 ratio leaves a different surplus of 10,000.

Keep the inputs alongside the ratio when comparing scenarios. A ratio by itself does not reveal the scale of the cash-flow difference.

Limits and terminology

Negative operating income is permitted and produces a negative ratio. Zero debt service leaves the ratio undefined, so this form requires a positive denominator. Definitions of qualifying income, reserves and debt service differ between analyses and lenders.

The tool does not review leases, vacancy, loan documents or lender underwriting. The OCC-hosted income property lending handbook explains the NOI/debt-service relationship. Use cap rate to compare operating income with property value instead of financing payments.
