Inputs and units

Use the units shown for Daily calorie target, Protein (%), Carbohydrates (%), Fat (%). Required fields cannot be blank. Use zero only where that field and the model allow it. Select Calculate after changing a scenario.

Method and assumptions

Protein, carbohydrate and fat percentages must total 100%. For each nutrient, multiply daily calories by its percentage. Divide protein and carbohydrate calories by 4 and fat calories by 9 to obtain grams.

Worked example

A 2,000-calorie allocation of 30% protein, 40% carbohydrates and 30% fat corresponds to 150 g protein, 200 g carbohydrates and about 66.7 g fat.

Limitations

The daily calorie target and percentages are user assumptions. The tool does not select an appropriate diet, account for medical conditions or model differences in food energy measurement.
