What this calculator estimates

Estimate retrieval, medication and storage costs for egg freezing.

Inputs and units

Fill in each field, then select Calculate to update the results. Required fields cannot be blank.

  • Number of retrieval cycles: the cycles you are planning for. It must be at least 1.
  • Clinic and retrieval cost per cycle ($): the clinic and retrieval price for one cycle.
  • Medication cost per cycle ($): the medication cost for one cycle.
  • Annual storage fee ($): the yearly storage fee.
  • Storage years: the years of storage to plan for.
  • Insurance or employer coverage (%): the percentage of the total you expect a plan or employer to cover, from 0 to 100.

Method and assumptions

The calculator multiplies the cycle count by the clinic plus medication cost for the treatment line, multiplies the annual storage fee by the storage years, adds both for the gross cost, and reduces the total by the coverage percentage.

With the illustrative starting inputs: the treatment line is 1 times 12,000 plus 5,000, which is $17,000.00, and storage is 800 times 5, which is $4,000.00. The gross is $21,000.00, and with coverage at 0% the total stays $21,000.00 and the coverage line shows $0.00.

Reading the result

  • The total cost is the headline: $21,000.00 on the starting inputs, one cycle plus five storage years with no coverage entered.
  • The treatment line ($17,000.00) scales with the cycle count; the storage line ($4,000.00) scales with the years entered.
  • The coverage percentage applies to the whole gross, treatment and storage alike; the starting inputs enter none, so the coverage value is $0.00.

Common mistakes

  • Entering a per-cycle clinic price that already includes medication and also filling the medication field; the model adds both, so the medication is counted twice.
  • Assuming the plan percentage applies; enter only coverage confirmed in your plan documents or by the insurer, since actual benefits depend on the plan's terms.
  • Reading the storage line as open-ended; the model stops at the years you enter.
  • Entering a lifetime storage fee in the annual field; the model multiplies the field by the storage years.

Frequently asked questions

What do multiple cycles look like?

The cycle count multiplies the clinic plus medication figure: 2 cycles on the starting inputs would make the treatment line $34,000.00.

Does storage extend automatically?

No. The storage-years field sets it, and the model multiplies the annual fee by exactly that figure.

What does the coverage percentage apply to?

The whole gross, treatment plus storage. What a plan actually covers depends on its own terms, so check your plan documents.

Important limitations

Testing, anesthesia, monitoring, future thawing, fertilization, transfer and travel may be billed separately.

Account for cycles and storage separately

Use the quoted cost for one retrieval cycle and the number of cycles being budgeted. Medication, testing and storage may be quoted separately from retrieval. Check whether the entered storage period is already included in a package. These costs do not estimate the number of eggs retrieved, future fertility outcomes or the later cost of using stored eggs.
