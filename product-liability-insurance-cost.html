What this calculator estimates

Estimate product-liability insurance cost from annual sales, a rate per $1,000, product risk, coverage limits, deductible adjustment and endorsements.

How to use it

Enter Annual product sales ($); Base rate per $1,000 of sales ($); Product risk factor; Coverage-limit factor; Deductible discount (%); Recall, worldwide or retailer endorsements ($); Policy fees ($). Select Calculate to update the results.

How the calculation works

Annual sales are converted to units of $1,000 and multiplied by the entered base rate, product-risk factor and coverage factor. Deductible discount, endorsements and fees are then applied.

Important limitations
