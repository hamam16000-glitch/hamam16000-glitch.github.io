    Using this calculator

    Enter each fraction as a separate numerator and denominator. Use signed whole numbers without decimal dots or scientific notation. A negative denominator is accepted and normalized to a positive denominator. Choose the operation before calculating; subtraction and division use the first fraction followed by the second.

    Calculation method

    a/b + c/d = (ad + bc) / bd
    a/b − c/d = (ad − bc) / bd
    (a/b)(c/d) = ac / bd
    (a/b) ÷ (c/d) = ad / bc

    After the operation, the numerator and denominator are divided by their greatest common factor. Browser BigInt arithmetic keeps these integer steps exact. The mixed-number view and an approximate decimal are shown separately so that a repeating decimal is not mistaken for an exact value.

    Worked examples

    Addition with different denominators

    1/2 + 1/3 = (3 + 2)/6 = 5/6. Its decimal is approximately 0.833333333333.

    A signed mixed number

    −7/3 × 1/1 remains −7/3. The mixed form is −2 1/3, meaning −(2 + 1/3).

    Understanding the result

    A whole-number result is displayed without “/ 1”. A zero numerator reduces to 0/1 internally. Reducing a fraction changes its representation, not its value. Mixed numbers are an alternative reading of the same signed result; type them into this form as improper fractions.

    Boundaries and assumptions

    Each input permits at most 18 digits plus a sign. Neither denominator may be zero, and the fraction used as a divisor must be nonzero. Fraction results are exact within those input limits. Decimal results use floating-point conversion and may round; they should not replace the exact fraction in further symbolic work.

    Questions and reference

    How do I enter 2 1/3?

    Convert it to 7/3 first: multiply the whole part by the denominator and add the numerator.

    Can I enter Arabic digits?

    Yes. Arabic and Persian integer digits are normalized before exact integer calculation.
