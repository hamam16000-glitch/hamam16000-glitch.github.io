What this calculator estimates

Add business days to a start date while skipping Saturdays and Sundays.

Inputs and units

Fill in each field, then select Calculate to update the results. Required fields cannot be blank.

  • Start year: the starting year, a whole value.
  • Start month: the starting month, from 1 to 12.
  • Start day: the starting day; the date must exist on the calendar.
  • Business days to add: the count of business days to move forward, a whole value. It can be 0.

Method and assumptions

The calculator walks forward from the start date, counting only weekdays until it has moved the entered number of business days, and reports the landing date, the calendar days that took, the weekend days skipped, and the landing weekday.

With the illustrative starting inputs: 10 business days after July 27, 2026 land on August 10, 2026, which is 14 calendar days later with 4 weekend days skipped, and the landing day is a Monday.

Worked example

Friday October 16, 2026 plus one business day is Monday October 19. Saturday October 17 plus five business days is Friday October 23. Entering zero leaves the starting date unchanged.

Reading the result

  • The headline is the landing date: 2026-08-10 on the starting inputs, ten weekdays after the start.
  • The calendar line (14 days) against the business count (10) shows the weekend effect directly: the 4 skipped days are the Saturdays and Sundays in between.
  • The weekday line (Monday) is a quick sanity check on the landing date, useful when the result feeds a schedule or a deadline.

Common mistakes

  • Entering calendar days instead of business days; the count field skips weekends, so enter the weekday count only.
  • Expecting holidays to be skipped; the model skips Saturdays and Sundays only, and public holidays are outside it.
  • Entering a fractional count; the business-day count is a whole number, and the form rejects anything else.

Frequently asked questions

How are weekends handled?

They are skipped in the count: 10 business days from 2026-07-27 span 14 calendar days because 4 weekend days fall in between.

Are public holidays skipped?

No. The model counts Saturdays and Sundays only; holiday calendars are outside it.

Can I count backward?

The field is for adding business days. Enter a negative count and the model walks backward, skipping weekends the same way.

Limitations

Public holidays, regional Friday/Saturday weekends and business-specific closures are not included. Output dates must fall between years 0100 and 9999.
