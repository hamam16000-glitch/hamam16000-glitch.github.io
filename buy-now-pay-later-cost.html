What this calculator estimates

Estimate installment size and total BNPL cost above cash price.

Inputs and units

Fill in each field, then select Calculate to update the results. Required fields cannot be blank.

  • Purchase price ($): the price of what you are buying. It cannot be negative.
  • Down payment ($): the part paid up front. It cannot be negative or exceed the price.
  • Remaining installments: the number of payments after the down payment, as a whole number of at least 1.
  • Plan or financing fee ($): the fee the plan charges. It can be 0.
  • Expected late fees ($): late charges you expect, included so the plan cost is not understated. It can be 0.

Method and assumptions

The calculator subtracts the down payment to get the financed amount, adds the plan and late fees, divides that across the installments for the payment size, then totals everything paid and expresses the cost above the purchase price as a percentage markup.

With the illustrative starting inputs: the financed amount is 1,200 minus 300, which is $900.00, and adding 60 of fees gives $960.00 to split over 6 installments at $160.00 each. Total paid is 300 plus $960.00, which is $1,260.00, so the plan costs $60.00 over the price, a 5% markup.

Reading the result

  • The installment is the headline: $160.00 on the starting inputs, six of them after the 300 down payment.
  • The total paid ($1,260.00) is the real commitment; the $60.00 above the price is the cost of using the plan.
  • The markup (5% here) prices the plan against the purchase; it ignores how long the installments run, so a longer plan at the same fee shows the same percentage.

Common mistakes

  • Entering the financed amount as the price; the model subtracts the down payment itself, so enter the full purchase price.
  • Forgetting that fees are spread into the installments; the payment covers principal plus every entered fee.
  • Reading the markup as an annual rate; it is a one-time percentage of the price, so a six-week plan and a six-month plan at the same fee show the same figure.
  • Comparing plans on the installment alone; two plans can share a payment size while differing on total cost, which the total and markup lines expose.

Frequently asked questions

How is this different from the per-payment view?

This page centers the cost: the total paid ($1,260.00 on the starting inputs) and the $60.00 and 5% above the price, so the plan's price is visible next to its installment.

Is the markup an interest rate?

No. It is the total cost above the price divided by the price, with no time element. Plans with the same fee show the same markup regardless of length.

What if the plan charges no fee?

Enter 0 and the model shows a markup of 0; the installments then just split the financed amount.

Important limitations

Promotional terms, refunds, merchant discounts, late-fee limits and credit reporting differ by provider.
