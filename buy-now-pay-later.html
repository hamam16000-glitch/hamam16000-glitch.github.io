What this calculator estimates

Estimate installment amount, fees and effective financing cost for a buy-now-pay-later purchase.

How to use it

Enter Purchase amount ($); Upfront payment ($); Remaining installments; Total plan fees ($); Expected late or service fees ($). Select Calculate to update the results.

How the calculation works

Remaining plan cost after the upfront payment is divided by the number of installments.

Worked example: starting scenario

Example inputs
Purchase amount ($)1200
Upfront payment ($)300
Remaining installments3
Total plan fees ($)36
Expected late or service fees ($)0
Example results
Estimated installment payment$312.00
Total amount paid$1,236.00
Total financing fees$36.00
Fees as % of purchase3%

Important limitations

Payment timing, late fees, merchant returns and credit reporting differ by provider.

Payment count is after the down payment

The remaining-installment count excludes the upfront payment. For a $1,200 purchase, $300 upfront, $36 total plan fees and three remaining installments, each installment is ($1,200 − $300 + $36)/3 = $312. The modeled total paid is $1,236. This arithmetic does not turn the fee into an annual percentage rate or model the timing of missed payments.
