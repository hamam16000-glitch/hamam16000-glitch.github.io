What this calculator estimates

Calculate current ratio and working capital. The calculation runs locally in your browser and is intended for planning and comparison.

How to use it

Enter the requested values, select Calculate, review the headline result and supporting figures, then change one input at a time to compare another scenario.

How the calculation works

The current ratio equals current assets divided by current liabilities. Working capital equals current assets minus current liabilities.

  • Current ratio = current assets ÷ current liabilities.
  • Working capital = current assets − current liabilities.
  • Assets needed for the target ratio = current liabilities × target ratio.
  • Target cushion = current assets − assets needed for the target ratio.

Example: With $450,000 in current assets, $275,000 in current liabilities, and a target ratio of 1.5, the current ratio is 1.64×, working capital is $175,000, and the business is $37,500 above the target.

Important limitations

The current ratio does not measure asset quality by itself. Slow-moving inventory, doubtful receivables, and payments due soon can make actual liquidity weaker than the ratio suggests.

Compare the result with similar businesses and with the same company's historical trend. Liquidity needs vary by industry, seasonality, and operating cycle.

Frequently asked questions

Is this result exact?

No. It is a planning estimate based on the values entered and the method described on the page.

Can I enter Arabic or Persian digits?

Yes. Arabic and Persian digits are normalized to English digits inside numeric fields.

Does the calculator store my inputs?

No. The calculation runs locally in your browser and does not require an account.
