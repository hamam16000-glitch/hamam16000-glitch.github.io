What this calculator estimates

Estimate a builders-risk insurance premium using project value, policy rate, construction duration, project risk, deductible adjustment and endorsements.

How to use it

Enter Completed project value ($); Annual premium rate (%); Construction duration (months); Project and location risk factor; Deductible discount (%); Flood, wind, delay and soft-cost endorsements ($); Policy and inspection fees ($). Select Calculate to update the results.

How the calculation works

Project value is multiplied by the annual rate and prorated for construction duration. Project risk, deductible adjustment, endorsements and fees are applied.
