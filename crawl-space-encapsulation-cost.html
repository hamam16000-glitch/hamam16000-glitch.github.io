What this calculator estimates

Estimate a crawl-space encapsulation project cost from area, liner, labor, drainage, dehumidification, mold treatment, permits and contingency.

Inputs and units

Fill in each field, then select Calculate to update the results. Required fields cannot be blank.

  • Crawl-space area (sq ft): the floor area to be encapsulated in square feet. It cannot be negative.
  • Liner and sealing materials per sq ft ($): vapor liner and sealing material cost per square foot.
  • Labor per sq ft ($): installation labor per square foot.
  • Drainage and sump work ($): drainage and sump-pump work as a project total.
  • Dehumidifier and electrical work ($): the dehumidifier and its electrical work.
  • Mold treatment and cleanup ($): treatment and cleanup needed before sealing.
  • Permits and inspection ($): permit and inspection fees.
  • Contingency (%): an allowance for unknowns, as a percentage of the subtotal.

Method and assumptions

The calculator multiplies the area by the combined liner and labor rates for the core encapsulation cost, adds drainage, dehumidifier, mold treatment and permits as system costs, applies the contingency percentage to that subtotal, and reports the total plus a cost per square foot.

With the illustrative starting inputs: the core cost is 1,200 times the combined 4.5 and 2.5 rates, which is $8,400.00, and the systems add 2,500 plus 1,500 plus 800 plus 200, which is $5,000.00. The subtotal is $13,400.00, the contingency is 10% of that, $1,340.00, the total is $14,740.00, and the unit rate is $14,740.00 over 1,200, which is $12.28 per square foot.

Reading the result

  • The project total is the headline: $14,740.00 on the starting inputs, or $12.28 per square foot.
  • The core liner-and-labor work ($8,400.00) scales with the area, while the systems line ($5,000.00) stays flat as entered.
  • The contingency ($1,340.00) is 10% of the $13,400.00 subtotal, so every other field moves it.

Common mistakes

  • Entering the crawl-space height instead of the floor area; the model prices the area the liner covers.
  • Putting mold treatment into the contingency as well as its own field; the model adds both, so it is counted twice.
  • Leaving the dehumidifier line at zero when the design includes one; the model only adds what you enter.
  • Reading the per-square-foot line as a market rate; it is your entered costs averaged over your area.

Frequently asked questions

Does the total include mold treatment?

Only if you enter it. The starting inputs carry $800.00 for mold treatment inside the $5,000.00 systems line.

What is the contingency for?

Work you cannot price up front. The field adds a percentage of the subtotal, $1,340.00 on the starting inputs.

Does the area mean floor only or walls too?

The model multiplies one area by the liner and labor rates, so enter the area the liner will actually cover.

Important limitations

Access, crawl height, water intrusion, structural repairs, mold, insulation removal, local labor, drainage and code requirements can change actual bids.
