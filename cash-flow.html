What this calculator estimates

Estimate net cash movement and ending cash from operating, investing and financing activity.

How to use it

Enter Operating cash inflows ($); Operating cash outflows ($); Net investing cash outflow ($); Net financing cash flow ($); Beginning cash balance ($). Select Calculate to update the results.

How the calculation works

Net operating cash flow equals operating inflows minus operating outflows. Investing outflow is subtracted, financing cash flow is added, and the change is applied to beginning cash.

Important limitations

Use signed investing and financing values consistently. This simplified planner does not replace a classified statement of cash flows.
