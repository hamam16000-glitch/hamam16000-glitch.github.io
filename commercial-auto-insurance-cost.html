What this calculator estimates

Estimate an annual commercial-auto insurance budget from vehicle count, base premium, driver risk, industry risk, deductible choices and endorsements.

How to use it

Enter Number of insured vehicles; Base annual premium per vehicle ($); Driver and claims risk factor; Industry and vehicle-use factor; Estimated deductible discount (%); Hired, non-owned and other endorsements ($); Policy and administrative fees ($). Select Calculate to update the results.

How the calculation works

Vehicle count is multiplied by the base premium and editable driver and industry factors. The deductible discount is subtracted before endorsements and fees are added.

Important limitations

Driver records, vehicle type, radius, cargo, state, limits, filings, claims and insurer underwriting determine actual premiums.
