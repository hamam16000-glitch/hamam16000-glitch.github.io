What this calculator estimates

Simplify an integer ratio and calculate its decimal and percentage relationship.

Inputs and units

Fill in each field, then select Calculate to update the results. Required fields cannot be blank.

  • First ratio value: the left side of the ratio. It must be an integer.
  • Second ratio value: the right side. It must be an integer and cannot be zero, as the limitations note says.

Method and assumptions

The calculator divides both values by their greatest common divisor to simplify the ratio, then shows the first value divided by the second as a decimal, as a percentage, and reports the scale factor removed, which is that greatest common divisor.

With the starting inputs 24 and 36: the greatest common divisor is 12, so the simplified ratio is 2:3, the decimal is about 0.6666666667 and the percentage is 66.67%.

Reading the result

  • Simplified ratio is the headline: the smallest whole numbers with the same relationship.
  • First divided by second expresses the ratio as one number; 0.6666666667 means the first value is two thirds of the second.
  • Scale factor removed is what both sides were divided by; multiplying the simplified ratio by 12 returns 24 and 36.

Common mistakes

  • Entering decimals or a zero second value; the limitations note requires integers and a nonzero second value.
  • Swapping the values when the relationship is directional; 2:3 and 3:2 are different ratios.
  • Reading 66.67% as exact; it is the rounded display of the repeating decimal the ratio produces.
  • Simplifying by hand with a partial factor; dividing 24 and 36 by 2 gives 12:18, which is true but not fully simplified.

Frequently asked questions

What if the values are already coprime?

The scale factor removed is 1 and the ratio comes back unchanged.

Can I compare more than two quantities?

Not on this page; reduce them pairwise, or divide all of them by their shared greatest common divisor yourself.

Why does the decimal repeat?

Two thirds is a repeating decimal, and the display shows a rounded slice of it, which the percentage line inherits.

Important limitations

Inputs must be integers and the second ratio value cannot be zero.

Part-to-part is different from part-to-whole

A 2:3 ratio means the first quantity divided by the second is 2/3, or about 66.67%. If those are the only two parts of a mixture, the first part’s share of the total is 2/(2+3) = 40%. This form reports the first divided by the second, not the share of their sum. It reduces integer components by their greatest common divisor.
