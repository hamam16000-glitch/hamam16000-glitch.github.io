What this calculator estimates

Calculate a triangle’s area, perimeter and classification from three side lengths.

How to use it

Enter Side a; Side b; Side c. Select Calculate to update the results.

How the calculation works

Area is calculated with Heron’s formula after validating the triangle inequality. The largest angle uses the law of cosines.

Important limitations

All side lengths must be positive and form a valid triangle.

Sides determine one triangle up to reflection

All three side lengths must be positive, and the sum of the two shorter sides must be greater than the longest side. Lengths 3, 4 and 5 give area 6, perimeter 12 and a largest angle of 90°. Scaling every side by 10 multiplies perimeter by 10 and area by 100. The largest-angle output belongs opposite the longest side; the shape classification uses relative floating-point tolerance.
