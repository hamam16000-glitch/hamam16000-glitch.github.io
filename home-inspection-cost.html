What this calculator estimates

Estimate a home-inspection budget including base inspection, size, age, radon, sewer scope, mold, pool and other add-ons.

Inputs and units

Fill in each field, then select Calculate to update the results. Required fields cannot be blank.

  • Base home inspection fee ($): the inspector's quoted base fee, covering up to the included square footage.
  • Property size (square feet): the home's size. Only area beyond the included amount adds the surcharge.
  • Square feet included in base fee: how much area the base fee covers, from the quote.
  • Surcharge per extra square foot ($): the per-foot charge beyond the included area.
  • Older-home or complexity surcharge ($): the fixed surcharge for older or more complex homes, per the quote.
  • Radon test ($): the add-on price for a radon test. Enter zero if skipped.
  • Sewer scope ($): the add-on price for a sewer scope. Enter zero if skipped.
  • Mold or air-quality testing ($): the add-on price for mold or air-quality testing. Enter zero if skipped.
  • Pool, spa or other specialty inspection ($): the add-on price for a pool, spa or other specialty inspection. Enter zero if not applicable.

Method and assumptions

The model adds a per-foot surcharge for area beyond the base-fee allowance plus the age or complexity surcharge, adds each testing and specialty add-on you price in, and totals them with the base fee. There is no contingency field; an inspection is a quoted service.

With the starting inputs: 500 square feet beyond the 2,000 included, at $0.12, adds $60.00, and the $125 age surcharge makes $185.00 in surcharges. Base fee $425.00, add-ons $700.00 for radon, sewer and mold testing, plus $225.00 for the specialty inspection, give a total of $1,535.00.

Reading the result

  • Base inspection fee is the anchor; surcharges and add-ons stack on it per the quote.
  • Environmental and sewer add-ons combines the three testing fields; zero the ones you skip.
  • The total is the price of the scope you entered; a cheaper inspection is often a smaller scope, which the limits section's note about scope and the absence of any guarantee of future condition speaks to.

Common mistakes

  • Entering the full home size where the quote asks for area beyond the included amount; the model computes the excess itself.
  • Leaving add-on tests at the defaults when you do not want them.
  • Comparing inspectors on the base fee alone when the included area and add-on prices differ.
  • Reading a passed inspection as a guarantee; the limits section says an inspection does not guarantee future condition.

Frequently asked questions

Which add-on tests should I include?

That depends on the property and your concerns, not on this page. Price the ones you plan to order and zero the rest; the total shows exactly what the chosen scope costs.

Why does size change the fee?

Many inspectors price by area because larger homes take longer. The surcharge applies only beyond the area included in the base fee.

What if my quote is one flat fee?

Put it in the base fee field and zero the surcharge and add-on fields.

Important limitations

Inspection scope, property type, location, age, outbuildings, systems, certifications, travel and local pricing vary. An inspection does not guarantee future condition.
