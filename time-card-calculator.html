Read the complete guide

What this calculator estimates

Total weekly work hours, regular hours, overtime hours and estimated gross pay. The calculation runs locally in your browser and is intended for quick reference.

How to use it

Enter the requested values, select Calculate, review the headline result and supporting figures, then change one input at a time to compare another scenario.

How the calculation works

Sum daily hours, separate hours above the entered threshold, and apply the entered overtime multiplier to overtime pay.

Important limitations

Overtime laws and payroll rules vary. The estimate excludes deductions, taxes, shift premiums and unpaid breaks not already removed.

Frequently asked questions

Is the result exact?

The calculator follows the formula shown on the page and rounds only for display. Results still depend on the values and units you enter.

Can I compare different scenarios?

Yes. Change one input at a time and calculate again to compare the effect.

Does the calculator store my inputs?

The calculation runs in your browser and does not require an account. Avoid entering unnecessary confidential information.
