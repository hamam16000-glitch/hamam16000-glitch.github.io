What this calculator estimates

Estimate a surrogacy journey budget after insurance, reimbursements and contingency.

How to use it

Enter Agency and coordination fees ($); Carrier compensation and expenses ($); Medical, fertility and insurance costs ($); Legal, escrow and travel costs ($); Insurance or employer reimbursement ($); Contingency (%). Select Calculate to update the results.

How the calculation works

Core agency, carrier, medical and legal costs are totaled, then contingency and reimbursements are applied.

Important limitations

Multiple cycles, complications, insurance exclusions and state law can materially change total cost.

Define the contingency base

Agency, carrier, medical and legal entries are added before the percentage contingency. The entered reimbursement is then subtracted from that gross budget. Check whether an agency package already includes legal work or travel, and whether reimbursements arrive after bills are paid. Neither the total nor the contingency predicts how many medical attempts will be needed.
