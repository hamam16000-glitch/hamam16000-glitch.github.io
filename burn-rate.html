What this calculator estimates

Estimate gross and net monthly burn, annualized burn and runway from cash, operating expenses, revenue and one-time spending.

How to use it

Enter Current cash balance ($); Monthly operating expenses ($); Monthly cash revenue ($); One-time costs during analysis period ($); Analysis period for one-time costs (months). Select Calculate to update the results.

How the calculation works

Gross burn includes recurring operating expenses and an allocated monthly share of one-time costs. Net burn subtracts recurring revenue.

Important limitations

This is a planning average and does not model payment timing, fundraising, seasonality, deferred revenue, working-capital swings or restricted cash.
