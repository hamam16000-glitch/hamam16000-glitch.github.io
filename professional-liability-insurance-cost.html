What this calculator estimates

Estimate a professional liability or errors-and-omissions insurance budget using revenue, staff, risk and coverage assumptions.

How to use it

Enter Annual professional-services revenue ($); Number of professionals; Base premium rate (% of revenue); Additional cost per professional ($); Practice risk multiplier; Coverage-limit multiplier; Deductible credit (%). Select Calculate to update the results.

How the calculation works

The estimate combines a revenue-based premium component with an editable per-professional component and applies risk, limit and deductible factors.

Important limitations

Actual E&O, malpractice and professional liability underwriting varies widely by occupation, services, claims history, contracts, territory, limits and retroactive date.
