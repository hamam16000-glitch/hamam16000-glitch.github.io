What this calculator estimates

Estimate gross and net rental yield, NOI and yield on total acquisition cost for a rental property.

How to use it

Enter Current property value or price ($); Closing and acquisition costs ($); Monthly market rent ($); Vacancy and credit loss (%); Annual operating expenses excluding debt ($); Annual other property income ($). Select Calculate to update the results.

How the calculation works

Annual rent is adjusted for vacancy and combined with other income. Operating expenses are subtracted for NOI, which is divided by value and acquisition cost.

Important limitations

Debt service, income tax, depreciation, capital improvements, reserves and changes in market value are not included in the yield calculation.
