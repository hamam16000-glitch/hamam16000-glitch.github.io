What this calculator estimates

Estimate a mobile-app project cost from platforms, design, development, backend, quality assurance, launch and contingency.

How to use it

Enter Platforms (iOS, Android or equivalent); Platform-specific setup per platform ($); Product and interface design hours; Design hourly rate ($); Application development hours; Development hourly rate ($); Backend, integrations and infrastructure setup ($); QA, security review and launch ($); Contingency (%). Select Calculate to update the results.

How the calculation works

The calculator adds platform-specific setup, design and development labor, backend and launch work, then applies contingency.
