What this calculator estimates

Estimate a rough heating and cooling capacity from floor area and basic adjustments.

How to use it

Enter Conditioned floor area (sq ft); Climate load factor (BTU per sq ft); Ceiling-height factor; Regular occupants. Select Calculate to update the results.

How the calculation works

Illustrative cooling load in BTU/h = conditioned square feet × entered BTU/h per square foot × ceiling factor, plus 600 BTU/h for each occupant above two. Divide by 12,000 to convert BTU/h to refrigeration tons. The displayed comparison range is 90% to 110% of that result.

Important limitations

This is a rough editable sizing rule, not a room-by-room load calculation. Its 10% range is a comparison band, not a measured confidence interval. Insulation, air leakage, sun exposure, humidity, windows, ducts and local design temperatures require a proper load assessment before selecting equipment.
