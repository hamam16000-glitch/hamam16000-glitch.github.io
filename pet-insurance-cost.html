What this calculator estimates

Estimate annual pet insurance reimbursements, owner out-of-pocket bills and total annual cost.

How to use it

Enter Annual insurance premium ($); Annual deductible ($); Reimbursement rate (%); Eligible annual veterinary bills ($); Annual reimbursement maximum ($). Select Calculate to update the results.

How the calculation works

Eligible bills above the deductible are multiplied by the reimbursement percentage and capped at the annual maximum. The owner cost adds premiums to unreimbursed bills.

Important limitations
