What this calculator estimates

Estimate non-economic damages using an adjustable multiplier.

How to use it

Enter Economic damages used for multiplier ($); Lost wages and income ($); Other economic damages ($); Non-economic damages multiplier; Claimant fault reduction (%); Attorney contingency fee (%); Case costs, liens and reimbursements ($). Select Calculate to update the results.

How the calculation works

Economic damages are added to a multiplier-based non-economic estimate, reduced for entered fault, then attorney fees, costs and liens are subtracted.

Important limitations

Settlement values vary widely by jurisdiction, evidence, policy limits, caps, causation, comparative fault and negotiation. This is not legal advice.

A multiplier is not evidence of an award

The output is an arithmetic scenario using the damages and multiplier entered. The calculator does not derive the multiplier from case facts, local law, liability, insurance limits or comparable decisions. Use it to understand how a proposed multiplier changes a total, not to describe a legally owed or likely settlement. Preserve the underlying bills and avoid entering the same loss twice in separate damages fields.
