What this calculator estimates

Estimate a cloud-infrastructure budget from compute, storage, data transfer, managed services and support.

How to use it

Enter Compute instances; Monthly compute cost per instance ($); Stored data (TB); Monthly storage cost per TB ($); Monthly outbound data transfer (TB); Data transfer cost per TB ($); Monthly databases and managed services ($); Support and operations (%). Select Calculate to update the results.

How the calculation works

The calculator totals monthly compute, storage, transfer and managed services, applies a support percentage and annualizes the result.

Reading the result

  • The headline is the modeled annual cost: $69,564.00 on the starting inputs, twelve times the monthly total.
  • The monthly line totals the entered components plus the entered support share: $5,797.00 on the starting inputs.
  • The component lines break out compute ($2,500.00), storage and transfer ($770.00), and managed services with support ($2,527.00) on the starting inputs.
  • The support share applies to everything before it: raising any component raises the support amount too.

Important limitations

Reserved pricing, autoscaling, regions, architecture, discounts, taxes, egress, backups, observability, support tier and usage variability can change actual bills.

Common mistakes

  • Entering yearly figures in the monthly fields; every cost field except the instance count is monthly, and only the headline is annual.
  • Forgetting that support compounds; the entered percent applies to the sum of all other components.
  • Reading the estimate as a provider quote; unit prices are entered assumptions, and provider pricing changes.
  • Mixing units between the TB fields and their per-TB rates; storage and transfer are entered separately with their own rates.

Frequently asked questions

How is the monthly total built?

Compute ($2,500.00) plus storage and transfer ($770.00) plus managed services ($2,000.00), then the entered 10% support share on that sum: $5,797.00 on the starting inputs.

What is in the services line?

The entered managed services plus the modeled support cost: $2,527.00 on the starting inputs.

Are these real cloud prices?

No. Every rate is an entered assumption; actual pricing comes from the providers' current price lists.
