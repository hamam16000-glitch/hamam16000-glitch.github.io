What this calculator estimates

Convert kilometers per hour into miles per hour, meters per second, knots and feet per second.

Inputs and units

Fill in each field, then select Calculate to update the results. Required fields cannot be blank.

  • Speed in kilometers per hour: the speed to convert. The pace line is Undefined at zero or negative speed, as the limitations note says.

Method and assumptions

The calculator divides the entry by 1.609344 for miles per hour, by 3.6 for meters per second, by 1.852 for knots and by 3.6 and 0.3048 for feet per second, then divides 60 by the entry for minutes per kilometer. The factors come from the exact definitions of the mile and the nautical mile.

With the starting input 100 kilometers per hour: that is 62.1371192237 miles per hour, 27.7777777778 meters per second, 53.9956803456 knots, 91.1344415281 feet per second and a pace of 0.6 minutes per kilometer.

Reading the result

  • Miles per hour is the headline; meters per second, knots and feet per second are the same speed in the units other fields use.
  • Knots are nautical miles per hour, which is why the figure differs from land miles per hour.
  • Pace inverts the speed: 0.6 minutes per kilometer is the time each kilometer takes at 100 kilometers per hour.

Common mistakes

  • Entering a speed with units typed in, like 100kph; enter the plain number only.
  • Reading knots as miles per hour; the two lines differ because a nautical mile is longer than a statute mile.
  • Entering zero and expecting a pace; the limitations note says pace is undefined at zero or negative speed, and the line shows Undefined.
  • Confusing pace with speed when comparing runs; a smaller pace is faster, while a larger speed is faster.

Frequently asked questions

Are the conversion factors exact?

Yes at the definition level: a mile is exactly 1609.344 meters, a nautical mile is exactly 1852 meters, and the hour-to-second factor is exact; only display rounding introduces approximation.

How do I convert minutes per kilometer back to speed?

Divide 60 by the pace: 60 divided by 0.6 returns 100 kilometers per hour, which is exactly what the pace line computes.

Why is feet per second included?

It is the customary unit for short-interval physics and ballistics work; it equals the meters-per-second value divided by 0.3048.

Important limitations

Pace is undefined at zero or negative speed.

Speed and pace are reciprocals

At 10 km/h the pace is 6 minutes per kilometer, while at 20 km/h it is 3 minutes per kilometer. A value of 0.6 min/km is 36 seconds per kilometer, not 60 seconds. The converter uses one international mile = 1.609344 km and one knot = 1.852 km/h. A nonpositive speed has no positive travel pace in this form.
