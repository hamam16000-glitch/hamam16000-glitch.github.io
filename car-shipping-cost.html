What this calculator estimates

Estimate a vehicle-shipping quote from distance, mileage rate, vehicle size, enclosed transport, urgency, fuel surcharge and added coverage.

Inputs and units

Fill in each field, then select Calculate to update the results. Required fields cannot be blank.

  • Shipping distance (miles): the distance between pickup and delivery. It must be above 0.
  • Base transport rate per mile ($): the carrier's base rate per mile.
  • Vehicle size and condition factor: a multiplier for the vehicle; 1.1 on the starting inputs means 10% above the base rate.
  • Enclosed-transport surcharge ($): a flat surcharge for enclosed transport, 0 for open transport.
  • Expedited pickup surcharge (%): a percentage of the base transport for faster pickup.
  • Fuel surcharge (%): a percentage of the base transport for fuel.
  • Additional cargo coverage and fees ($): extra coverage and fixed fees.

Method and assumptions

The calculator multiplies distance by the per-mile rate and the vehicle factor for the base transport, applies the expedited and fuel percentages to that base for the surcharges, adds the enclosed and coverage amounts as flat add-ons, and reports the total plus an effective cost per mile.

With the illustrative starting inputs: the base is 1,500 times 0.75 times 1.1, which is $1,237.50. The combined 20% surcharge rate applies to that base and gives $247.50, the add-ons are 0 plus 150, which is $150.00, the total is $1,635.00, and the effective rate is $1,635.00 over 1,500, which is $1.09 per mile.

Reading the result

  • The shipping total is the headline: $1,635.00 on the starting inputs, or $1.09 per mile.
  • The base ($1,237.50) already carries the 1.1 vehicle factor, so the surcharges are computed on the factor-adjusted figure.
  • Both percentage surcharges multiply the base only, not each other and not the flat add-ons; that is how this model is built.

Common mistakes

  • Entering the vehicle factor as a percentage; the field is a multiplier, so 10% above base is 1.1, not 10.
  • Applying the surcharges to the total in your head; the model applies them to the base transport line only.
  • Reading the effective per-mile line as the carrier's quote; it averages your entered surcharges and fees over the distance.
  • Leaving the coverage field at zero when you want extra cargo coverage; the model adds only what you enter.

Frequently asked questions

Open or enclosed transport?

Enter the enclosed surcharge a carrier quotes; the model adds it as a flat amount. It is $0.00 on the starting inputs, which prices open transport.

Are the surcharges compounded?

No. Both percentages multiply the base transport line, so 15% expedited plus 5% fuel is a combined 20% of $1,237.50, which is $247.50.

Does the route affect the rate?

Not in the model. It applies your entered per-mile rate to your entered distance; any route pricing sits inside the rate the carrier gives you.

Important limitations

Season, route balance, pickup flexibility, vehicle condition, enclosed versus open carrier, fuel, location access and broker or carrier pricing affect quotes.
