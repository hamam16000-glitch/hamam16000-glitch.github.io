What this calculator estimates

Estimate annual home-warranty cost and compare it with expected repair spending using plan premiums, service fees, coverage percentage and exclusions. It is designed for fast planning and comparison in the United States. The output is an estimate, not a binding quote, approval, tax determination, insurance offer or professional recommendation.

How to use it

Enter the requested values using a single consistent time period and currency. Select Calculate, review the headline result and supporting figures, then change one assumption at a time to compare another scenario.

How the calculation works

Plan premium, add-ons, service fees, uncovered repair share and exclusions are combined. The same expected repairs without coverage provide a comparison.

Important limitations

Coverage caps, waiting periods, denied claims, appliance age, contractor availability, exclusions and actual repair frequency determine value.

Frequently asked questions

Is the result exact?

No. It is a planning estimate based on the assumptions entered.

Can I compare different scenarios?

Yes. Change one input at a time so you can see which assumption changes the result.

Does the calculator store my inputs?

The calculation runs in the browser and does not require an account. Avoid entering unnecessary confidential information.
