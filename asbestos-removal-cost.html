What this calculator estimates

Estimate an asbestos-abatement project cost from affected area, removal rate, testing, containment, disposal, clearance and contingency.

How to use it

Enter Affected area (sq ft); Removal and abatement per sq ft ($); Initial inspection and laboratory testing ($); Containment and air controls ($); Transport and hazardous disposal ($); Clearance testing and documentation ($); Contingency (%). Select Calculate to update the results.

How the calculation works

The estimate multiplies affected area by the entered abatement rate, adds testing, containment, disposal and clearance costs, then applies contingency.

Important limitations
