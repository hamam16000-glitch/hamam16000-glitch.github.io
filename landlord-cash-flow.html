What this calculator estimates

Estimate monthly and annual landlord cash flow after vacancy, expenses and debt service.

How to use it

Enter Monthly scheduled rent ($); Other monthly income ($); Vacancy and credit loss (%); Monthly operating expenses ($); Monthly mortgage payment ($). Select Calculate to update the results.

How the calculation works

Effective income applies vacancy loss to scheduled rent and other income. Cash flow subtracts operating costs and mortgage payment.
