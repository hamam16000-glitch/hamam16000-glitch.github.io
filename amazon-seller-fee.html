What this calculator estimates

Estimate Amazon referral, fulfillment, storage and other per-order fees plus expected net proceeds from a sale.

How to use it

Enter Item selling price ($); Shipping charged to customer ($); Referral fee (%); Fulfillment fee ($); Allocated storage or prep fee ($); Other per-order fee ($); Advertising cost as % of sale. Select Calculate to update the results.

How the calculation works

The calculator applies editable percentage fees to the sale amount and adds entered fulfillment, storage, advertising and other fees.

Important limitations

Fee schedules change and may differ by product category, dimensions, fulfillment method, country, promotions, refunds and seller plan.
