What this calculator estimates

Estimate hair transplant cost from graft count and price per graft.

How to use it

Enter Number of grafts; Cost per graft ($); Travel, medications and follow-up ($); Discount or insurance coverage (%); Financing and payment-plan fees ($). Select Calculate to update the results.

How the calculation works

Quantity is multiplied by the entered unit cost, fixed costs are added, and the selected discount or coverage is subtracted before financing fees.

Important limitations

Prices vary by provider, location, treatment plan, package terms, insurance and financing.

Grafts and package quotations

A per-graft quote must be multiplied by graft count, not by the number of individual hairs. If a provider quotes a complete package, avoid adding included facility and professional fees again. Travel, follow-up visits and revision procedures may need a separate allowance. The total does not predict hair growth or establish whether a procedure is suitable.
