What this calculator estimates

Estimate a small employer’s annual health-insurance cost from employee count, premiums, employer contribution, administration and potential credits.

Inputs and units

Fill in each field, then select Calculate to update the results. Required fields cannot be blank.

  • Covered employees: how many employees the plan covers. It must be greater than zero.
  • Average monthly premium per employee ($): the average monthly premium across covered employees.
  • Employer contribution (%): the share of the premium the employer pays, from 0 to 100.
  • Monthly administration cost per employee ($): the monthly cost of administering the plan per employee.
  • Estimated annual credits or subsidies ($): any annual credits or subsidies applied against the total. The model caps them at the combined cost, so the total cannot go below zero.

Method and assumptions

The calculator totals the employer's premium share across employees for the year, adds a year of per-employee administration, and subtracts credits for the estimated annual employer cost. It also shows the monthly equivalent and the premium and administration components separately.

With the illustrative starting inputs: 12 employees at 750 a month with a 70% employer share gives a premium cost of $75,600.00 a year, and administration at 25 per employee per month adds $3,600.00. With credits at 0, the estimated annual employer cost is $79,200.00, about $6,600.00 per month.

Reading the result

  • Estimated annual employer cost is the headline; Monthly equivalent divides it by 12.
  • Premium cost and Administration cost show the split; on these inputs premiums dominate at $75,600.00 against $3,600.00 of administration.
  • The limitations note says group rates, participation rules, age bands and credit eligibility can materially change actual cost, so confirm figures with the plan and a broker or licensed professional.

Common mistakes

  • Entering the employee's share as the premium; the premium field is the full per-employee premium, and the employer share percentage splits it.
  • Forgetting administration is per employee per month; it scales with both headcount and the 12 months here.
  • Assuming credits apply automatically; the model subtracts only what you enter, and eligibility for credits is outside the model, as the limitations note says, so verify with official sources.
  • Entering annual premiums in the monthly field; that inflates the premium cost twelve-fold.

Frequently asked questions

Is this an actual group-plan cost?

No. Group rates, participation rules, plan design, age bands, geography, dependents, broker fees and taxes can materially change actual cost, as the limitations note says; this is a budgeting model to review against real quotes and plan documents.

Can the total go below zero with large credits?

No; the model caps the applied credits at the combined premium and administration cost, so the annual total floors at $0.00.

Does this include what employees pay?

Only indirectly: the employer share percentage sets the employer's part of the premium. The employees' share is the rest, which this page does not total separately.

Important limitations

Group rates, participation rules, plan design, age bands, geography, dependents, broker fees, taxes and eligibility for credits can materially change actual cost.
