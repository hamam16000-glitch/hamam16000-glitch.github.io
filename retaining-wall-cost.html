What this calculator estimates

Estimate a retaining-wall project cost from wall area, material, excavation, drainage, engineering and contingency.

How to use it

Enter Wall face area (square feet); Installed wall cost per square foot ($); Excavation and access cost ($); Drainage and backfill cost ($); Engineering and permit cost ($); Contingency (%). Select Calculate to update the results.

How the calculation works

Wall area is multiplied by the installed unit cost, then excavation, drainage, engineering and contingency are added.
