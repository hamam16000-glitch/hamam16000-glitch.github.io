What this calculator estimates

Estimate move-in deposit requirements from rent, deposit multiplier, pet deposit and fees.

Inputs and units

Fill in each field, then select Calculate to update the results. Required fields cannot be blank.

  • Monthly rent ($): the monthly rent. It can be 0.
  • Security-deposit months: the deposit size in months of rent. It can be 0.
  • Pet deposit ($): any refundable pet deposit. It can be 0.
  • Other refundable deposits ($): any other refundable amounts. It can be 0.
  • Nonrefundable move-in fees ($): fees that are not returned. It can be 0.

Method and assumptions

The calculator totals the refundable side as the deposit months times the rent plus the pet and other deposits, adds the nonrefundable fees for the full move-in cost, and shows the refundable part, the fees, and the refundable part expressed in months of rent.

With the illustrative starting inputs: the refundable side is one month of the 2,200 rent plus the 300 pet deposit, which is $2,500.00, the fees add $250.00, the total move-in cost is $2,750.00, and the refundable part equals 1.14 months of rent.

Reading the result

  • The headline is the total due at move-in: $2,750.00 on the starting inputs, the $2,500.00 refundable side plus the $250.00 of fees.
  • The months line (1.14 months) restates the refundable total in rent terms, useful for comparing against deposit limits expressed in months.
  • Only the fee line is unrecoverable in this model; the refundable side comes back under the rental agreement's own terms, which govern any deductions.

Common mistakes

  • Entering the deposit as dollars in the months field; the deposit size is entered in months of rent, and the model multiplies.
  • Mixing nonrefundable fees into the deposit fields; the model keeps the fee line out of the refundable total.
  • Reading the split as a legal limit; the model totals the entered amounts, and any caps on deposits or fees follow the applicable local rules and the lease, so confirm with them.

Frequently asked questions

What counts as refundable here?

The deposit months times rent plus the pet and other deposit fields: $2,500.00 on the starting inputs. The 250 of fees stays outside it.

Why show the refundable part in months?

Deposit limits are often expressed in months of rent, so 1.14 months makes the comparison direct.

Does the model judge whether the fees are allowed?

No. It totals the entered amounts; what may be charged follows the applicable local rules and the lease terms.

Important limitations

Deposit caps, interest and refund rules vary by state and local law.

Interest is not a legal entitlement calculation

Use the interest method, years and rate shown by this form for a numerical scenario. Lease rules can specify another rate, a different interest period, deductions or a deadline. The result does not establish that interest is owed or that an entered deduction is permitted; compare it with the contract and applicable local requirements.
