What this calculator estimates

Estimate monthly and total COBRA continuation coverage cost from employee and employer premium shares.

Inputs and units

Fill in each field, then select Calculate to update the results. Required fields cannot be blank.

  • Current employee monthly premium share ($): what you currently pay each month toward the health plan.
  • Current employer monthly premium share ($): what the employer currently pays each month toward the same plan.
  • Administrative fee (%): the percentage added on top of the combined premium for administration.
  • Months of continuation coverage: how many months of continuation to total. It must be at least 1.

Method and assumptions

The calculator adds the employee and employer shares for the full monthly premium, applies the administrative fee percentage for the monthly continuation cost, then multiplies by the months for the total. It also shows how much more per month that is than the current employee share.

With the illustrative starting inputs: 250 plus 750 gives a full premium of $1,000.00, the 2% fee adds $20.00, so the monthly continuation cost is $1,020.00. Over 18 months the total is $18,360.00, and the monthly increase over the current employee share is $770.00.

Reading the result

  • Estimated monthly cost is the headline, the full premium plus the administrative fee.
  • Total continuation cost scales the monthly figure by the months entered; $18,360.00 over the illustrative 18.
  • Increase over current share shows the budget jump from paying the employee share to paying the full premium plus fee; $770.00 more per month here.

Common mistakes

  • Entering only your current share in both fields; the continuation cost builds on the combined employee plus employer premium.
  • Entering the fee as a decimal like 0.02 instead of 2; the field takes a percent.
  • Assuming eligibility or duration from this page; the limitations note says eligibility, maximum duration and state rules vary and directs you to the plan administrator.
  • Reading the monthly figure as a plan quote; it is the arithmetic on your entered shares, and the actual plan premium comes from the plan administrator.

Frequently asked questions

Who qualifies for continuation and for how long?

That depends on eligibility rules, maximum durations and state continuation rules, which this page does not model; the limitations note directs you to the plan administrator for those answers.

Why add the employer share?

Because continuation typically shifts the full premium to you, plus an administrative fee; the calculator mirrors that by combining both shares before applying the fee percentage.

Can I compare against a marketplace plan?

Yes: run this page for the continuation total, then compare the monthly figure against a marketplace quote's premium, keeping in mind coverage details differ.

Important limitations

Eligibility, maximum duration, disability extensions, state continuation rules and actual plan premiums vary. Confirm figures with the plan administrator.
