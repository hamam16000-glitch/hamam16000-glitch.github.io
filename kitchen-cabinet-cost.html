What this calculator estimates

Estimate kitchen cabinet replacement cost from linear footage, cabinet grade, installation, demolition, hardware, finishing and contingency.

Inputs and units

Fill in each field, then select Calculate to update the results. Required fields cannot be blank.

  • Cabinet run (linear ft): the total cabinet length in linear feet, measured along the walls getting cabinets.
  • Cabinet material cost per linear ft ($): the per-foot price for the cabinet line you plan. Stock, semi-custom and custom price very differently, as the limitations note says.
  • Installation labor per linear ft ($): the per-foot installation labor rate.
  • Old cabinet removal and disposal ($): the fixed amount for removing and disposing of existing cabinets. Enter zero for new construction.
  • Hardware and accessories ($): the fixed total for pulls, organizers and accessories.
  • Trim, panels, paint and touch-up ($): the fixed amount for trim, end panels and paint or touch-up work.
  • Design, delivery and permits ($): the fixed total for design services, delivery and any permits.
  • Contingency (%): a percentage buffer on the subtotal for wall or floor surprises behind old cabinets.

Method and assumptions

The model prices cabinet materials and installation labor per linear foot, adds removal, hardware, finishing and design amounts as fixed costs, then applies contingency.

With the starting inputs: 28 feet at $650 gives $18,200.00 in materials, 28 at $180 gives $5,040.00 in labor, removal, hardware, finishing and design add $6,700.00, and the $29,940 subtotal at 10 percent contingency adds $2,994.00, for a total of $32,934.00.

Reading the result

  • Cabinet materials is the dominant line and moves directly with the per-foot rate you choose.
  • Installation labor scales with the same footage; long runs cost more to hang.
  • Removal, hardware and finishing combines four fixed fields, so check each when the line looks off.

Common mistakes

  • Measuring wall length where no cabinets go, such as window spans the quote excludes.
  • Comparing a stock per-foot rate against a semi-custom quote's total; match the rate to the cabinet line.
  • Forgetting delivery or design fees when the quote lists them separately.
  • Counting hardware inside a bundled cabinet price and again in the hardware field.

Frequently asked questions

Where do plumbing or electrical changes go?

There is no field for them; the limitations note lists them as pricing factors. Add the quoted amount to the design, delivery and permits field as a fixed cost.

How do I compare stock versus semi-custom?

Run the calculator once per cabinet line with each line's per-foot material rate. Labor usually stays similar; the material line carries the difference.

Why is my total so different from a showroom estimate?

Check whether the showroom figure included installation, removal, hardware and delivery. This model prices each separately so nothing is hidden in a per-foot rate.

Important limitations

Stock, semi-custom or custom construction, wood species, layout, appliance panels, delivery, plumbing and electrical changes affect pricing.
