Read the complete guide

What this calculator estimates

Calculate a z-score, cumulative percentile, upper-tail probability and density. The calculation runs locally in your browser and is intended for quick reference and comparison.

How to use it

Enter the requested values, select Calculate, review the headline result and supporting figures, then change one input at a time to compare another scenario.

How the calculation works

Standardize x with z = (x − mean) ÷ standard deviation, then evaluate the normal cumulative distribution.

Important limitations

The percentile uses a numerical approximation to the error function and represents an ideal normal distribution.

Frequently asked questions

Is the result exact?

The calculator follows the stated formula and rounds only for display. Results can still depend on the quality and meaning of the values you enter.

Can I compare different scenarios?

Yes. Change one input at a time to see how it affects the result.

Does the calculator store my inputs?

The calculation runs in your browser and does not require an account. Avoid entering unnecessary confidential information.
