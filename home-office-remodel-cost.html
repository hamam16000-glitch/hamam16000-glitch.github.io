What this calculator estimates

Estimate home-office remodeling cost from room area, finishes and wiring.

How to use it

Enter Office square feet; Low cost per unit ($); High cost per unit ($); Electrical, data and built-ins ($). Select Calculate to update the results.

How the calculation works

The estimate multiplies the quantity by the low and high unit-cost assumptions, adds the fixed costs, and shows the midpoint of that range.

Important limitations

Actual project pricing varies by location, materials, labor, access, permits, contractor minimums and scope changes. Use local written quotes before committing.

Interpreting the low/high midpoint

Use office floor area for the unit costs. Keep electrical circuits, data wiring and custom built-ins in the fixed allowance only if the square-foot quotation excludes them.

Low total = quantity × low rate + fixed costs; high total = quantity × high rate + fixed costs. The displayed midpoint is the average of those totals. It is a scenario midpoint, not a statistical estimate or a prediction that the final bill will land there. Raising fixed costs changes both ends equally; raising a unit rate changes only its corresponding end.
