What this calculator estimates

Estimate a radon-mitigation project cost from base system pricing, foundation zones, crawl-space work, electrical service, testing and permits. It is designed for fast planning and comparison in the United States. The output is an estimate, not a binding quote, approval, tax determination, insurance offer, legal conclusion, medical recommendation or professional advice.

How to use it

Enter the requested values using a single consistent time period and currency. Select Calculate, review the headline result and supporting figures, then change one assumption at a time to compare another scenario.

How the calculation works

The calculator starts with a base active system, adds extra suction zones and crawl-space work, then includes electrical, testing and permit costs.

Important limitations

Foundation type, number of zones, crawl spaces, routing, electrical access, local code, radon level and contractor warranty affect actual pricing.

Frequently asked questions

Is the result exact?

No. It is a planning estimate based on the assumptions entered.

Can I compare different scenarios?

Yes. Change one input at a time so you can see which assumption changes the result.

Does the calculator store my inputs?

The calculation runs in the browser and does not require an account. Avoid entering unnecessary confidential information.
