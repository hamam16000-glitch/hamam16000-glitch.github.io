What this calculator estimates

Estimate the first-year cost of a home security system including equipment, sensors, installation, monitoring and activation.

Inputs and units

Fill in each field, then select Calculate to update the results. Required fields cannot be blank.

  • Base hub and equipment package ($): the price of the base kit or hub package, from the provider's quote.
  • Number of additional sensors or cameras: how many extra sensors or cameras beyond the base package you plan.
  • Average cost per sensor or camera ($): the average per-device price for the extras.
  • Professional installation ($): the one-time installation charge. Enter zero for self-install.
  • Monthly monitoring fee ($): the monthly monitoring price from the actual plan terms, including any contract obligations that come with it.
  • Months of monitoring included: how many months of monitoring to model in the total. Use 12 for a first-year view.
  • Activation and permit fees ($): the one-time activation and any local alarm permit fees.

Method and assumptions

The model adds the base package to the extra devices, adds installation and activation as one-time setup costs, then adds the monthly fee times the entered months for a first-period total. It also repeats the monthly fee as the ongoing cost line.

With the starting inputs: base $650 plus 8 devices at $125 gives $1,650.00 in equipment, installation and activation add $475.00, and 12 months at $45 adds $540.00, for a first-year total of $2,665.00. Ongoing cost after that is the $45.00 monthly fee.

Reading the result

  • Equipment and sensors is the one-time hardware line; the device count moves it directly.
  • Monitoring cost for entered period is the only recurring line; the total is a first-period view, not a lifetime cost.
  • Estimated ongoing monthly cost is just the monitoring fee you entered; contract terms and price changes live in the plan documents, not here.

Common mistakes

  • Reading the total as the full cost of ownership; it covers only the entered months, and later years add monitoring again.
  • Ignoring contract length and cancellation fees; the limitations note flags them, and they are in the plan's terms, not this model.
  • Counting a bundled free-installation offer in the installation field anyway.
  • Entering a promotional monthly fee that expires; the ongoing line will repeat whatever you enter.

Frequently asked questions

Why model months of monitoring at all?

So the first-year total includes both one-time and recurring costs. After the entered period, the ongoing monthly line is the cost that repeats.

Where do cloud storage or cellular backup fees go?

If they are part of the monitoring plan, they belong inside the monthly fee you enter. If they are separate subscriptions, add them to the monthly fee so the ongoing line reflects the real recurring total.

How do I compare self-monitoring?

Set the monthly fee to zero and the months to any value; the total then shows equipment plus setup only. Whether self-monitoring meets your needs is outside this model.

Important limitations

Contracts, financing, cellular backup, cloud storage, permits, discounts, smart-home integrations and cancellation fees vary.
