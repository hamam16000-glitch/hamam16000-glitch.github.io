What this calculator estimates

Estimate travel insurance cost as a percentage of insured trip cost plus traveler surcharges, optional coverage and policy fees.

Inputs and units

Fill in each field, then select Calculate to update the results. Required fields cannot be blank.

  • Total insured trip cost ($): the prepaid trip cost you want to insure. It must be greater than zero.
  • Base premium rate (%): the percentage of trip cost charged as the base premium.
  • Number of travelers: how many travelers; each adds the flat surcharge. It must be at least 1.
  • Surcharge per traveler ($): the flat amount added per traveler.
  • Optional coverage add-ons ($): flat add-ons for optional coverages.
  • Policy fee ($): a flat policy fee.

Method and assumptions

The calculator applies the base rate to the trip cost, adds the per-traveler surcharge for each traveler, then adds the optional add-ons and the policy fee. It also shows the cost per traveler and the premium as a percentage of trip cost.

With the illustrative starting inputs: the base premium is 6,000 times 6%, which is 360, and 2 travelers at 35 each add 70. With 120 of add-ons and a 25 fee, extras total $215.00, and the estimated total premium is $575.00, $287.50 per traveler, an effective rate of 9.58% of trip cost.

Reading the result

  • Estimated total premium is the headline; Per-traveler cost divides it across the entered travelers.
  • Extras total collects everything beyond the base rate: the surcharges, add-ons and fee.
  • Effective rate restates the total against the trip cost; 9.58% against the 6% base rate here, and the limitations note says age, destination and pre-existing-condition waivers affect actual quotes, so read the policy exclusions.

Common mistakes

  • Reading the effective rate as the insurer's rate; it restates your own inputs after the flat extras, which push 6% to 9.58% here.
  • Entering the surcharge once instead of per traveler; the model multiplies it by the traveler count.
  • Assuming the add-ons cover any cancellation reason; the limitations note says cancellation benefits and exclusions affect real coverage, and the policy documents define what is covered.
  • Forgetting the flat fee; the 25 sits outside the percentage math and is easy to overlook in a manual estimate.

Frequently asked questions

Is this an actual travel-insurance quote?

No. Age, destination, trip duration, pre-existing-condition waivers, coverage limits and cancellation benefits affect actual quotes, as the limitations note says, and it advises reading exclusions carefully; this is a budgeting model.

Why is the effective rate higher than the base rate?

Because the per-traveler surcharges, add-ons and fee stack on top of the percentage: here they add $215.00 to a 360 base, lifting the total to 9.58% of the trip cost.

Does the per-traveler cost mean each traveler gets a separate policy?

No; it is just the total divided by the traveler count for budgeting. How a policy covers multiple travelers is defined by the policy itself.

Important limitations

Age, destination, trip duration, pre-existing-condition waivers, coverage limits and cancellation benefits affect actual quotes. Read exclusions carefully.
