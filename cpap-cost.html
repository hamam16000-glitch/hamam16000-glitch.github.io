What this calculator estimates

Estimate first-year out-of-pocket CPAP equipment and supply costs.

How to use it

Enter Number of services or units; Low allowed cost per service ($); High allowed cost per service ($); Facility or professional fees ($); Insurance coverage after deductible (%); Deductible still owed ($). Select Calculate to update the results.

How the calculation works

The calculator estimates a low and high allowed charge, applies the remaining deductible first, then applies the entered insurance coverage percentage to the remaining amount.

Reading the result

  • The headline is the midpoint of the modeled out-of-pocket range: $845.00 on the starting inputs.
  • The range lines bracket it: $650.00 - $1,040.00 modeled out of pocket across the low and high allowed costs, against $1,000.00 - $2,300.00 of entered charges.
  • The deductible is applied first, then the entered coverage share pays the entered percentage of the rest: 70% on the starting inputs.
  • The figures model cost-sharing mechanics only; actual allowed amounts and coverage come from the insurer and plan documents.

Important limitations

Medical prices and insurer-negotiated allowed amounts vary widely. This is not a benefit determination, diagnosis or medical advice. Confirm coverage and pricing with the provider and insurer.

Common mistakes

  • Entering a coverage share as if it applied before the deductible; the model applies the deductible first, then the coverage percentage.
  • Entering a high cost below the low cost; the page requires the high to be at least the low.
  • Reading the midpoint as a quote; it averages the two modeled ends of the entered range.
  • Leaving the deductible at the starting value; the field is the deductible still owed, and a met deductible changes the result.

Frequently asked questions

How is the midpoint computed?

Out of pocket is computed at the low and high allowed costs ($650.00 and $1,040.00 on the starting inputs); the headline averages them: $845.00.

What does the coverage percentage do?

After the deductible still owed is subtracted, the plan is modeled as paying that share of the remainder: 70% on the starting inputs.

Where do real figures come from?

Allowed amounts, deductibles, and coverage are set by the insurer and the plan; this page models only the arithmetic of the entered figures.

Device purchase versus recurring supplies

Put the machine and setup charge in the one-time fields, then use the replacement frequency assumed for masks, tubing and other supplies. A rental-to-purchase arrangement can bill differently from an outright purchase. The arithmetic does not verify equipment coverage, medical necessity, compliance requirements or a supplier agreement.
