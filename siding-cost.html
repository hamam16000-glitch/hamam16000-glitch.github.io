What this calculator estimates

Estimate siding materials, labor, removal and permits from exterior wall area and waste allowance.

How to use it

Enter Exterior wall area (square feet); Waste allowance (%); Siding material per square foot ($); Installation labor per square foot ($); Old siding removal per square foot ($); Permit and inspection costs ($). Select Calculate to update the results.

How the calculation works

Exterior area is increased by the waste percentage, then multiplied by material, installation and removal rates before adding permits.
