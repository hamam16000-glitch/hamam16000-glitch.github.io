What this calculator estimates

Estimate an umbrella liability coverage target from net worth, income exposure and existing liability limits.

How to use it

Enter Current net worth ($); Annual income ($); Years of future income to protect; Existing home and auto liability limit ($). Select Calculate to update the results.

How the calculation works

The planning target combines current net worth with selected future income exposure, subtracts existing liability coverage and rounds the remaining gap to a common umbrella-policy increment.

Important limitations

This is not a coverage recommendation. Risks such as rental properties, pools, boats, teen drivers, business activity and policy exclusions require an insurance professional.
