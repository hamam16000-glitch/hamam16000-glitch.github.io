What this calculator estimates

Estimate online degree tuition, aid and loan interest.

How to use it

Enter Credits required; Tuition per credit ($); Technology, books and other fees ($); Grants, scholarships and employer aid ($); Share of net cost financed (%); Loan APR (%). Select Calculate to update the results.

How the calculation works

Tuition equals credits times cost per credit plus fees; aid is subtracted and the financed share is amortized over ten years.

Reading the result

  • The headline is the modeled net cost: $48,000.00 on the starting inputs, the gross cost minus the entered aid, floored at zero.
  • The gross line totals tuition and fees: $60,000.00 on the starting inputs; the aid line shows the entered $12,000.00.
  • The loan line applies the entered financed share to the net cost: $33,600.00 on the starting inputs.
  • The last line is the modeled loan interest over ten years at the entered APR: $13,214.94 on the starting inputs.

Important limitations

Transfer credits, tuition changes, living expenses, loan fees, deferment and tax benefits are not included.

Common mistakes

  • Entering a per-year tuition in the per-credit field; tuition is priced per credit, and the credit count multiplies it.
  • Reading the financed share as a recommendation; the model applies the entered share to the net cost mechanically.
  • Comparing the net cost with the loan total without noting the interest line; borrowing adds the modeled interest.
  • Treating the figures as an aid offer; actual costs, aid, and loan terms come from the school and the lender.

Frequently asked questions

How is the net cost computed?

Credits times per-credit tuition plus fees, minus aid: $60,000.00 - $12,000.00 = $48,000.00 on the starting inputs.

Where does the interest line come from?

The financed share is amortized over ten years at the entered APR: $33,600.00 at 7% costs $13,214.94 in interest on the starting inputs.

Is this a real aid or loan offer?

No. It models the entered figures; actual pricing, aid, and loan terms come from the school and the lender.

Credits, fees and living costs

Check whether tuition is quoted per credit, per term or for the entire program. Convert it to the unit shown in the form before adding technology, books or other fees. Housing and living costs should represent the extra cost caused by study rather than expenses that would exist unchanged without enrolling.
