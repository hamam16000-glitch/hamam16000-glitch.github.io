What this calculator estimates

Estimate a chimney-repair budget from masonry area, liner, cap, scaffolding, inspection, permits and contingency.

How to use it

Enter Masonry repair area (sq ft); Masonry repair per sq ft ($); Flue liner repair or replacement ($); Cap, crown and flashing work ($); Scaffolding and roof access ($); Inspection, permits and cleanup ($); Contingency (%). Select Calculate to update the results.

How the calculation works

The estimate totals area-based masonry work, liner and cap components, access and inspection costs, then applies contingency.
