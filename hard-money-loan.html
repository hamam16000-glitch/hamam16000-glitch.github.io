What this calculator estimates

Estimate a hard-money loan amount, cash required, interest-only payment, points and total financing cost for a property project.

How to use it

Enter Property purchase price ($); Planned rehab cost ($); Maximum financing (%); Annual interest rate (%); Loan term (months); Lender points (%). Select Calculate to update the results.

How the calculation works

The estimated loan equals total purchase and rehab cost multiplied by the entered financing percentage. Interest is modeled as interest-only, with points charged on the initial loan amount.

Important limitations

Hard-money underwriting often uses purchase-price LTV, after-repair value, draw schedules and reserves rather than one simple percentage. Appraisal, inspection, legal and extension fees are not included.

Loan-to-cost is the model used here

The percentage input is applied to purchase price plus rehab cost. It is therefore a loan-to-project-cost assumption, not a loan-to-appraised-value calculation. For $100,000 purchase plus $50,000 rehab at 80%, the modeled loan is $120,000 and the project cash gap is $30,000 before points. A lender using after-repair value, holdbacks or staged draws can produce a different advance.
