What this calculator estimates

Estimate monthly Shopify store profit, contribution margin and break-even revenue from sales, product cost, payments, ads, apps and refunds.

How to use it

Enter Monthly gross sales ($); Orders per month; Product cost (% of sales); Payment processing rate (%); Fixed payment fee per order ($); Refund allowance (% of sales); Advertising spend ($); Shopify plan and apps ($); Other monthly overhead ($). Select Calculate to update the results.

How the calculation works

Gross sales are reduced by product cost, payment processing, fixed transaction fees and refunds to estimate contribution. Advertising, platform and overhead costs are then subtracted.

Important limitations

Taxes, shipping subsidies, chargebacks, discounts, inventory write-offs, customer support, fulfillment and attribution can materially change store profitability.
