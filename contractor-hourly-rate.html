What this calculator estimates

Convert an employee-equivalent salary into a contractor billing rate using benefits, taxes, overhead, utilization and profit margin.

How to use it

Enter Comparable employee salary ($); Benefits value (% of salary); Employer payroll taxes (%); Business overhead (% of direct cost); Billable hours per week; Billable weeks per year; Target profit margin (%). Select Calculate to update the results.

How the calculation works

Salary is increased for benefits and employer taxes, then for business overhead. The resulting annual cost is divided by billable hours and grossed up for target margin.

Important limitations

Market rates, insurance, travel, equipment, downtime, payment terms, taxes and contract risk can require a different rate.
