What this calculator estimates

Estimate net proceeds from selling a home after mortgage payoff, agent compensation, seller closing costs, repairs and other expenses.

Inputs and units

Fill in each field, then select Calculate to update the results. Required fields cannot be blank.

  • Expected sale price ($): the price the home is expected to sell for. It must be above 0.
  • Mortgage payoff ($): the remaining loan balance to pay off. It can be 0.
  • Agent compensation (%): the agent's share of the sale price. It can be 0.
  • Other seller closing costs (%): other closing costs as a share of the price. It can be 0.
  • Repairs and concessions ($): repairs and concessions as a flat amount. It can be 0.
  • Other selling costs ($): any other flat selling costs. It can be 0.

Method and assumptions

The calculator prices the agent compensation and the other closing costs as their percentages of the sale price, adds the flat repairs, concessions and other costs, then subtracts the total and the mortgage payoff from the price. It also shows the equity before selling costs and each cost line.

With the illustrative starting inputs: the equity before costs is 550,000 minus 275,000, which is $275,000.00, the agent line is $27,500.00, the closing line is $8,250.00, the total selling cost is $50,750.00, and the net proceeds are $224,250.00.

Reading the result

  • The headline is the estimated net proceeds: $224,250.00 on the starting inputs, what remains of the $275,000.00 equity after $50,750.00 of selling costs.
  • The percentage lines scale with the price: on a higher sale price the agent and closing percentages grow in step, while the flat repair and other lines stay fixed.
  • The payoff is subtracted at face value; the actual payoff figure comes from the lender's own statement, which can differ from a remaining-balance estimate.

Common mistakes

  • Entering the agent percentage as a dollar amount; the two cost-rate fields are percentages of the sale price, while the other two cost fields are flat dollars.
  • Forgetting the flat cost lines; the model adds repairs, concessions and other costs on top of the percentage lines, and leaving them out overstates the proceeds.
  • Reading the estimate as a closing figure; actual closing statements follow the transaction's own documents, so confirm with the settlement agent's figures.

Frequently asked questions

What is the equity line?

The price minus the payoff before any selling costs: $275,000.00 on the starting inputs. The selling costs then take $50,750.00 to reach the $224,250.00 net.

Do the percentage costs move with the price?

Yes. The 5% agent line is $27,500.00 and the 1.5% closing line is $8,250.00 only at the 550,000 price; both scale with it.

Does the model include taxes on the sale?

No. It nets the selling costs and payoff only; any tax on the gain is a separate question for the applicable tax rules.

Important limitations

Actual payoff statements, taxes, transfer charges, liens, prorations, commissions and concessions vary by property and location. This is not a settlement statement.
