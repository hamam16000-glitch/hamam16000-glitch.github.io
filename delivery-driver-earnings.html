What this calculator estimates

Estimate delivery-driver net earnings after mileage costs.

Inputs and units

Fill in each field, then select Calculate to update the results. Required fields cannot be blank.

  • Completed orders: the deliveries finished in the period. It can be 0.
  • Average base pay per order ($): the platform pay per order before tips. It can be 0.
  • Average tip per order ($): the average tip per order. It can be 0.
  • Miles driven: the total miles driven for the work in the period. It can be 0.
  • Vehicle cost per mile ($): your estimate of fuel and wear per mile. It can be 0.
  • Hours worked: the hours worked in the period. It must be above 0.

Method and assumptions

The calculator multiplies the orders by base pay plus tips for the gross, subtracts the miles times the per-mile vehicle cost, and reports the net, the net per hour, the gross, and the vehicle cost.

With the illustrative starting inputs: 90 orders at 6.5 plus 4.5 each give a gross of $990.00, the 520 miles at 0.4 a mile cost $208.00, so the net is $782.00, which is $26.07 per hour over 30 hours.

Reading the result

  • The headline is the net: $782.00 on the starting inputs, what the gross leaves after the entered vehicle cost.
  • The per-hour line ($26.07 / hour) is the comparable figure across weeks; it falls when the same net spreads over more hours.
  • The split between gross ($990.00) and vehicle cost ($208.00) shows what driving itself consumes; raising the per-mile estimate lowers the net one-for-one.

Common mistakes

  • Entering total pay instead of per-order averages; the model multiplies by the order count, so totals get counted again.
  • Using the vehicle's fuel-only cost; the per-mile field is your own estimate of fuel plus wear, and understating it overstates the net.
  • Counting only logged delivery hours; the model divides by the hours you enter, so include waiting time if you want the net per hour actually worked.

Frequently asked questions

Are tips included?

Yes, through the average tip per order: 90 times 4.5 is part of the $990.00 gross on the starting inputs.

Does the vehicle cost use an official rate?

The model uses the per-mile figure you enter as your own estimate of fuel and wear; any tax deduction follows the tax authority's own rules, so confirm those separately.

Can hours be zero?

No. The per-hour line divides by the hours worked, so enter hours above 0.

Important limitations

Taxes, platform adjustments, insurance and unpaid time are excluded.

Revenue and vehicle cost use the same shift

Match delivery revenue, tips, miles and working hours to the same period. Time spent waiting or returning without an order still affects earnings per hour. An entered cost per mile can represent fuel alone or a broader vehicle allowance; avoid adding the same fuel cost again. The result does not determine employee status or tax deductions.
