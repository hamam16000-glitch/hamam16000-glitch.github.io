What this calculator estimates

Estimate a basement-waterproofing project cost from perimeter drainage, sump pumps, wall sealing, exterior work, permits and contingency.

How to use it

Enter Interior drainage perimeter (linear ft); Drainage system per linear ft ($); Sump pumps; Installed cost per sump pump ($); Wall sealing area (sq ft); Wall sealing per sq ft ($); Exterior excavation and grading ($); Permits and electrical work ($); Contingency (%). Select Calculate to update the results.

How the calculation works

The calculator totals perimeter drainage and pumps, area-based wall sealing, exterior work and permits, then adds contingency.
