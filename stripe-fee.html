What this calculator estimates

Estimate Stripe processing, refund and dispute fees for one or many transactions and the net amount received.

How to use it

Enter Average transaction amount ($); Number of successful transactions; Processing rate (%); Fixed fee per transaction ($); Refunded transactions; Nonrefundable fee per refund ($); Disputes or chargebacks; Fee per dispute ($). Select Calculate to update the results.

How the calculation works

The calculator multiplies payment volume by the entered percentage rate, adds fixed per-transaction fees and then includes entered refund and dispute fees.

Important limitations

Domestic, international, card-present, ACH, currency conversion, platform, dispute and custom pricing can differ. Use the current pricing agreement.

Why changing refund count can leave the fee total unchanged

Refund count multiplies the separately entered extra fee per refund. With that fee set to zero, changing the refund count adds no extra fee, while the original transaction-processing charges remain in the fee estimate. The net-received output is explicitly before refunded principal: it does not subtract the amount returned to customers or disputed principal. Use the actual fee terms for your account; the default percentage and fixed charge are examples.
